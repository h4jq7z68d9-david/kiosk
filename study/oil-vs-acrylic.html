Oil vs Acrylic

A short course comparing oil and acrylic paint.

How oil and acrylic paint differ: what is in each, how each dries, how they handle and layer, and how they age. Concept pages explain one idea with an original diagram; example pages show it in a public-domain painting or in David Nicholson's own acrylic work.

  • Use the outline, the arrow keys, or Prev/Next to move between pages.
  • Click any image to see it full screen.
  • Use Bookmark to save a page, and Saved (top right) to find your saved pages again.
  • Diagrams and the acrylic examples are David Nicholson's own work; the other artworks are public domain. Sources and licenses are listed under Image credits.
