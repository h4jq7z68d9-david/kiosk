Value and Light

A short course on value and light in drawing and painting.

How light and dark build form in drawing and painting: the value scale, what one light does to a solid, kinds of light, edges, the great styles of light and shade, and ways to work in value. Concept pages explain one idea, mostly with plates from two old public-domain drawing books (Mary Merrifield, 1880; Anson Cross, 1897); example pages show it in a public-domain work.

  • Most concept images are plates from Merrifield's Handbook of Light and Shade (1880) and Cross's Light and Shade (1897), both public domain; the artworks are public domain. Sources are listed under Image credits.
