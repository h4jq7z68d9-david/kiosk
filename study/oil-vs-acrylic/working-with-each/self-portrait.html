Working With Each

Rembrandt van Rijn

Self-Portrait

1659

Oil on canvas, 84.5 × 66 cm. National Gallery of Art, Washington

The thickest paint sits where light hits the face.

  • The lights went in first as stubby dabs of heavy paint.
  • After they dried, he went back over them with small touches of darker color that he left unblended.
  • In the hair, lines were scratched into the wet paint with the end of the brush.
  • The background and hands are thin by contrast.
