Working With Each

Claude Monet

Stacks of Wheat (End of Summer)

1890–91

Oil on canvas, 60 × 100.5 cm. The Art Institute of Chicago

Monet used both timings in one canvas: some passages worked wet, others painted over dry paint.

  • Look at the edges of the large stack: paint built up wet-in-wet.
  • In the sky, zigzag strokes were blended wet, then more color went on once they had dried.
  • Some thick strokes look slightly flattened, possibly while still soft; the signature went on over dry paint.
