Two Paints Compared

Impasto

Impasto is paint applied so thickly that it rises off the surface and holds the shape of each brush or knife stroke.

Look at

  • Where the lavender swirls cross the white, they sit on top as raised bands instead of mixing into it.
  • Fine grooves left by the bristles run through each swirl, and the paint folds over into small lips where the brush lifted away.

Rembrandt van Rijn

Lucretia, 1664

The National Gallery of Art finds extensive palette-knife work in her left cuff and in the lower part of her dress.

Some oil colors are stiff enough from the tube to hold peaks on their own; acrylic texture is often built with gels or pastes, thickened mediums with fillers such as calcite or silica.
