Impasto

Impasto is paint laid on thickly enough to hold the shape of each brush or knife stroke. The raised ridges catch light and cast small shadows, so the strokes themselves show.

Image: National Gallery of Art, Washington · Public domain (NGA Open Access, CC0)

The National Gallery of Art finds extensive palette-knife work in her left cuff, at the right of the picture, and low on her dress, where thick, broken pale patches catch the light.
