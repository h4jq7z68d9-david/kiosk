Two Paints Compared

Acrylic Dries

Acrylic dries by losing water: as it leaves, the plastic droplets crowd together and fuse into a film.

Look at

  • In the middle panel the water line has dropped and the droplets are pressed against one another.
  • In the last panel they have become one film, thinner than the stack of droplets it came from.

David Nicholson

Early, Still, 2025

Each flat block set quickly enough for the next to go straight over it, and no color underneath has smudged into the layer above it.

For about a week after it feels dry, sometimes longer, water can still soften acrylic; dried in the cold, the droplets merge poorly and leave a porous or powdery film.
