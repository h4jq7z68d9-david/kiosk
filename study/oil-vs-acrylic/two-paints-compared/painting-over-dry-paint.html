Painting Over Dry Paint

Once a layer is dry, new paint no longer mixes with it, so the layer can be changed or covered completely; such changes are called pentimenti, and some show through later. In this unfinished altarpiece, a dog’s head from an earlier idea shows faintly above the angel’s knee.

X-rays show a double portrait of a man and a woman underneath, turned on its side; Titian kept the man's jacket and made it her red velvet wrap.
