Two Paints Compared

Painting Over Dry Paint

Once a layer is dry it can be changed or covered completely, and changes hidden this way are called pentimenti.

Look at

  • The two red shapes underneath are complete forms of their own, hidden by a single later layer.
  • The dashed lines turn solid where they enter the top layer, and their arrowheads stop just short of the red shapes.

Titian

Venus with a Mirror, about 1555

X-rays show a double portrait of a man and a woman underneath, turned on its side; Titian kept the man's jacket and turned it into the red velvet wrap over her hip.

Oil paint may be laid over acrylic, but acrylic should not be put over oil.
