Choosing Between Them

Neither paint is better; each suits a different way of working.

Choose by how you like to paint and where the work will live.

  • To blend and rework over days, pick oil.
  • To build crisp layers fast and clean up with water, pick acrylic.
  • To use both, keep thin acrylic underneath and oil on top, on an acrylic ground.
