Color Theory

A short course on color for painters.

How color works on the canvas: what light and the eye do, how colors are named and measured, how they change one another, and how pigments behave. Concept pages explain one idea with a historic plate or an original diagram; example pages show it in a public-domain painting.

  • Use the outline, the arrow keys, or Prev/Next to move between pages.
  • Click any image to see it full screen.
  • Use Bookmark to save a page, and Saved (top right) to find your saved pages again.
  • Diagrams are original to this site or historic public-domain plates; paintings are public domain or openly licensed. Sources and licenses are listed under Image credits.
