Arranging a Picture

Composition

Composition is how a painter arranges the parts of a picture. In 1435 Leon Battista Alberti named it one of three parts of a painter's job and compared the picture to an open window. In his 1899 book Composition, the American teacher Arthur Wesley Dow drew heavily on Japanese art, with line, dark-and-light and color as its elements.
