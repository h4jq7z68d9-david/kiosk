Foundations

Edgar Degas

A Woman Seated beside a Vase of Flowers (Madame Paul Valpinçon?)

1865

Oil on canvas, 73.7 × 92.7 cm. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

The woman is pushed to the right and cut off by the edge, while a large bouquet takes the center.

  • Look at the lower right corner: only a sliver of her hand is left in.
  • Met curator Alison Hokanson describes her as "squished in at the right."
  • She argues Degas planned such skewed layouts to give an effect of chance.
