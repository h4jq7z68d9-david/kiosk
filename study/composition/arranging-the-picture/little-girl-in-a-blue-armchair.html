Arranging the Picture

Mary Cassatt

Little Girl in a Blue Armchair

1878

Oil on canvas, 89.5 × 129.8 cm. National Gallery of Art, Washington

The big blue chair and the sprawling girl sit right of center; a small dark dog in a matching chair and an open stretch of floor answer them at left.

  • Cassatt moved the dog from the floor up to the chair, the National Gallery of Art found.
  • She wrote that Degas advised her; he added the room's corner, a wide diagonal that deepens the space.
