Symmetric Balance

Balance is how a picture's parts are spread so it feels steady. Symmetric balance mirrors one side in the other around a central axis.

Poore drew a center line through Detaille's scene to show two nearly equal groups. Dow called symmetry "the most common and obvious way of satisfying the desire for order."

  • Dow noted it turns up in "cheap and mean design" where beauty of form is ignored.
  • Poore linked strict, equal balance more with decoration than with pictures.
