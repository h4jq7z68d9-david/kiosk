Laziness (La Paresse)

1896

Woodcut, image 17.8 × 22.2 cm. The Art Institute of Chicago

There is no gray here: only black ink and bare paper.

  • The Art Institute notes that black and white alone give him pattern, space and solid form.
  • Look at the pale body: it is mostly bare paper, shaped by the black around it.
