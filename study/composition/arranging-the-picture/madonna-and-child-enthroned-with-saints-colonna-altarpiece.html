Arranging the Picture

Raphael

Madonna and Child Enthroned with Saints (Colonna Altarpiece)

ca. 1504

Oil and gold on wood, main panel 172.4 × 172.4 cm. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Look for the mirror: the Virgin sits on the central axis, with God the Father centered above.

  • Two female saints flank the throne; two weighty male saints stand in front, one on each side.
  • In the arched panel on top, angels and seraphim flank God the Father in the same way.
