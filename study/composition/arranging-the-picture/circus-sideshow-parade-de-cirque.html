Arranging the Picture

Georges Seurat

Circus Sideshow (Parade de cirque)

1887–88

Oil on canvas, 99.7 × 149.9 cm. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Golden-ratio claims about this painting come from measuring it. Roger Fischler's 1981 study found no sign Seurat used the ratio.

  • Look at the stiff horizontals, verticals and rows of lights: easy to fit a grid to, which is why overlays tempt.
  • Simple ratios such as 5:8 sit close to the golden ratio, so a near fit proves little.
  • His real planning shows in his studies for La Grande Jatte; see Checking a Composition.
