Arranging a Picture

Armatures

An armature is a basic figure, such as a triangle, a circle, an S-curve, a cross or an L, that the main masses of a picture are built around.

Look at

  • Under each emblem along the top, small sketches show pictures built on that shape.
  • In the landscape row the triangle lies on its side, its point running away into the distance along a road.

The Holy Family with the Young Saint John the Baptist, ca. 1528
