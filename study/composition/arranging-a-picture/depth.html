Arranging a Picture

Depth

Depth cues, such as overlap and linear perspective, make a flat surface look deep.

Look at

  • Two huge gate posts stand in front and cut across everything behind them.
  • Two walkers by the building are drawn large; the figures far back at right are small.

Utagawa Hiroshige

Plum Estate, Kameido, 1857

The plum trunk is so close it runs off the frame, and the orchard and its small visitors are seen through its branches, with almost nothing in between.

Linear perspective, worked out by Brunelleschi around 1420 and explained by Alberti, runs receding lines to one vanishing point level with the viewer's eye.
