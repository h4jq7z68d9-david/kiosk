Arranging a Picture

Format

Format is the outline of the whole picture, tall, wide, square or round, and one subject feels different in each.

Look at

  • One house and one tree are redrawn in three frames: tall and narrow, nearly square, and a wider upright.
  • The narrow version lets the tree dominate, with bare ground filling most of its lower half.
  • In the two squarer frames the house claims far more of the space.

Arthur Wesley Dow

View of Ipswich, or A Bend in the River, ca. 1895

The Met links this tiny upright print to Japanese pillar prints; its narrow frame stacks the view in bands, from field to houses to river to hills.

A picture is also a flat surface, and its pattern of shapes can pull against the illusion of space, says Smarthistory's Steven Zucker.
