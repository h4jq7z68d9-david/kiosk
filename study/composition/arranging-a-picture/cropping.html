Arranging a Picture

Cropping

Cropping decides which parts of a scene the frame keeps, and where its edges slice through what remains.

Look at

  • One stand of trees is framed five ways: one nearly square, two tall and two wide.
  • Each frame cuts the trunks and treetops at a different place, so each makes a different picture.

Edgar Degas

A Woman Seated beside a Vase of Flowers (Madame Paul Valpinçon?), 1865

The bouquet takes the center while the woman is pushed against the right edge and cut off by it; a Met curator argues Degas planned such layouts to look like chance.

The Met notes that Degas took bold cropping from Japanese prints, and from sixteenth-century painters who sometimes left a figure partly outside the picture.
