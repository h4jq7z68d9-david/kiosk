Leading the Eye

Painters plan a route for the eye: a way in, stops, and a way out. Bright spots, strong contrasts, and openings pull the eye, so their placement can lay out the route.

Image: Project Gutenberg · Public domain

Window light falls on the white headdress and collar, the pitcher, and the basin; a Met audio-guide speaker says it "is guiding me around the room."
