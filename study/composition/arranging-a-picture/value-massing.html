Arranging a Picture

Value Massing

Value massing groups the light and dark parts of a picture into a few big shapes, so the design reads at a glance; Dow called this notan, Japanese for "dark-light."

Look at

  • Corot's landscape at lower right is one great dark mass of tree with a pale gap of sky below it.
  • Manet's beach at top left is the reverse: a pale field holding one large dark shape.

Katsushika Hokusai

Under the Wave off Kanagawa (The Great Wave), ca. 1830–32

Seen as dark and light, the print comes down to a few big shapes: the deep blue body of the wave, the pale sky, and Fuji's white peak against a band of gray.

To Dow, notan was a pattern the painter designs, separate from the light and shadow that fall on things in nature.
