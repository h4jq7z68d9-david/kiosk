Asymmetric Balance

Asymmetric balance pairs two unlike sides that still feel level. It works like a seesaw: a large shape near the center can be balanced by a small one far out toward the edge.

Image: Project Gutenberg · Public domain

Cassatt answers the large chair and sprawled girl right of center with a small dark dog and open floor at left, which keep the right from outweighing the left.
