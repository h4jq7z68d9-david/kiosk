Arranging a Picture

Focal Point

The focal point is where the eye lands first, and painters build it with contrast, isolation and placement.

Look at

  • A single dark lion lies alone in a pale, nearly empty desert.
  • The other darks, a low strip on the horizon and a few scrubby plants at lower right, are thin and broken by comparison.

Caravaggio

The Denial of Saint Peter, 1610

Lit faces and hands stand out against near-black darkness, and the Met reads the pointing fingers as three accusations, matching Peter's three denials.

The English artist J. T. Smith advised in 1797 that a picture should never hold two equal main lights: one should lead and the rest give way.
