The Painter's Colors

Titian

Venus and Adonis

about 1555–60

Oil on canvas, 160 × 196.5 cm. J. Paul Getty Museum, Los Angeles

Both kinds of red sit side by side: vermilion in Adonis's orange-red sleeve, a red lake in his pinker tunic and in the cloth over Venus's seat.

  • The lake contains textile fibers. It was probably made from clippings of red-dyed cloth.
  • Tests matched the dye to cochineal, with some madder-type colorant too.
