What Paintings Are Made Of

Yellows

The most brilliant yellows before 1800 were often poisonous, and one of the vivid new yellows of the 1800s grew out of a red-orange ore.

Look at

  • Left: orpiment, an arsenic mineral, bright yellow where it is pure and gray where iron pyrites runs through it.
  • Right: crocoite, natural lead chromate; in its red-orange crystals the chemist Vauquelin found chromium in 1797, and by 1809 he had made the same compound in the laboratory as chrome yellow.

Vincent van Gogh

Sunflowers, 1888

Chrome yellows in several shades, from a pale lemon to a deep orange, supply most of the painting's yellows and oranges.

Cennini says orpiment looks "like gold in colour" and tells painters not to let it near their mouths.
