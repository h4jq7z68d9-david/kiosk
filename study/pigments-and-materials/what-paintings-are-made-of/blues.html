Blues

Before 1700 painters had only a few blues. Two were crushed minerals: ultramarine, from the stone lapis lazuli, and azurite, from a copper ore.

Image: Smithsonian Libraries via Internet Archive · Public domain

Vermeer painted the turban's blue in top-quality natural ultramarine, with lead white in the light and chalk in the shade, so one blue turns from pale to deep around the head.
