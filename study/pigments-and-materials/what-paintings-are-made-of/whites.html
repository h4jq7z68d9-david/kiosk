What Paintings Are Made Of

Whites

Until about 1900 the painter's main white was lead white, made by letting the fumes of vinegar eat into lead until it turned to a white crust.

Look at

  • Left: a disc of lead, pierced with openings so more of the metal is exposed to the fumes.
  • Right: the pot it sat in. Acetic acid, the acid in vinegar, filled the narrow lower part.
  • The discs rested on the shoulder above, bathed in fumes but clear of the liquid.

Rembrandt van Rijn

Self Portrait at the Age of 63, 1669

On the lit cheek, lead white tinted with ochres stands up in a thick layer over brown paint below.

Lead white is toxic; in 1978 the United States outlawed lead in paint for homes.
