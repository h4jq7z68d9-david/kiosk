What Paintings Are Made Of

Encaustic

Encaustic paint is pigment carried in beeswax; the word comes from the Greek for "burnt in".

Look at

  • Warm wax brushes on like paint and stiffens as it cools, which is why most encaustic painters kept their colors hot.
  • Going back over cooled strokes with a heated tool softens them again so they can be smoothed.

Roman Egypt

Portrait of a young woman in red, 90–120 CE

The wax went directly onto a board of linden only millimeters thick, and a hot tool left rows of parallel marks beside the nose.

Roughly 1,000 portraits like this survive from Roman Egyptian mummies, most from the 1st to 3rd centuries CE.
