What Paintings Are Made Of

Greens

Many old greens came from copper: malachite, a crushed mineral, and verdigris, made by exposing copper to vinegar.

Look at

  • Malachite forms crusts of rounded green knobs; the fine stippling gives them the downy look Sowerby thought more like a plant than a mineral.
  • Pale rims ring the knobs where the crust has broken open.

Paolo Uccello

The Battle of San Romano, probably about 1438–40

The hedge behind the knights started out green; the museum records that it has turned nearly black.

Cennino Cennini, in his painters' handbook of about 1400, told painters to keep verdigris and lead white apart, calling them "mortal enemies".
