Oil

Oil paint holds its pigment in a drying oil, most often linseed, which sets slowly by absorbing oxygen.

Image: The Met Open Access · Public domain (CC0)

Tests identified linseed oil; the lips melt together where wet paint met wet paint, and the brush pulled through the fur trim before it dried.
