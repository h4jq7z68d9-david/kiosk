What Paintings Are Made Of

Egg Tempera

Egg tempera uses yolk to hold the pigment, and it was the standard paint for Italian panels through the Middle Ages and early Renaissance.

Look at

  • Egg paint dries too quickly to blend smoothly, so fine parallel strokes curve along the Child's arm and legs instead, tracing their roundness.
  • Shadows come from packing those strokes closer and darker, not from softening their edges.
  • Behind the Child, the background is gold leaf, with his halo punched into it in rings of tiny dots.

Giotto

Madonna and Child, about 1310–15

Beneath the pink of the flesh lies a green underpaint that sets the shadows before any warm color goes on.

By the late 1400s Italian painters often combined tempera with oil, and after about 1500 tempera became less common.
