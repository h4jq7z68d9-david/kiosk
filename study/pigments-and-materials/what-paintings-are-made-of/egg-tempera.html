Egg Tempera

In egg tempera yolk holds the pigment, making a fast-drying paint laid on in thin strokes; it was the standard paint for Italian panels through the Middle Ages and early Renaissance.

Image: The Metropolitan Museum of Art Open Access · CC0 (Met Open Access)

Giotto set the shadows in a green underpaint before the pink of the flesh went on; it shows through as a cool gray-green on the shaded sides of the faces.
