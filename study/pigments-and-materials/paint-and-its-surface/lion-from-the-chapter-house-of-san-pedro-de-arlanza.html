Lion, from the Chapter House of San Pedro de Arlanza

after 1200

Fresco, mounted on canvas, 332.7 × 335.3 cm. The Metropolitan Museum of Art (The Cloisters), New York

This lion was painted on a monastery wall, taken down in the early twentieth century and mounted on canvas.

  • Lifting paintings off walls goes back to at least the 1500s.
  • Other wall paintings from the same monastery are in Barcelona, at the Museu Nacional d'Art de Catalunya.
