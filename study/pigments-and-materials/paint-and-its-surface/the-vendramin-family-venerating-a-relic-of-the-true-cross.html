Paint and Its Surface

Titian

The Vendramin Family, venerating a Relic of the True Cross

about 1540–45

Oil on canvas, 206.1 × 288.5 cm. The National Gallery, London

Nearly 3 m wide, this canvas is a single piece with no seam, woven in a patterned twill.

  • Patterned cloth like this was woven far wider than a meter.
  • It has been lined, backed with a second canvas, probably three times, most recently with wax-resin in 1961.
