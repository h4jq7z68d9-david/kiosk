Paint and Its Surface

Peter Paul Rubens

Samson and Delilah

about 1609–10

Oil on oak, 185 × 205 cm. The National Gallery, London

Over the white chalk ground, Rubens brushed a streaky yellow-brown layer, and he let it show.

  • In the folds of Delilah's white sleeve he left the layer bare as a warm half-shadow.
  • The layer is an earth color like umber, with a little lead white, in oil.
  • A 1983 National Gallery study by Joyce Plesters traced the layers, down to the six oak planks.
