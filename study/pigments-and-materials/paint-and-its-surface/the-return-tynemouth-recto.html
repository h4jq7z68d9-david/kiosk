Paint and Its Surface

Winslow Homer

The Return, Tynemouth (recto)

1881

Transparent and opaque watercolor with gum glaze over graphite on paper, 34.2 × 34.3 cm. Art Institute of Chicago, Chicago

Look at the pale pink in the clouds and on the boat. Homer used colors that resist light here; in many of his other watercolors, similar washes made with less stable reds have faded.

  • The museum lists rewetting, blotting and scraping on this sheet: ways of taking color off the paper to win back lights.
