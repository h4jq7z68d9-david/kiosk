Light, Paint and the Eye

Color and Emotion

Colors carry mood: strong, warm colors tend to read as lively or tense and cool, dull ones as quiet or sad, though viewers differ.

Look at

  • Goethe and Schiller divided a ring of colors among the four temperaments, written around the rim.
  • Orange and orange-red fall to the choleric temperament, blue to the phlegmatic.
  • An inner ring pairs each color with a kind of person, from tyrants and heroes to poets and teachers.

Edvard Munch
