Light, Paint and the Eye

Saturation

Saturation is the distance between a color and a gray of the same lightness.

Look at

  • Each row of chips starts at the gray column and steps outward, every chip stronger than the one before.
  • The rows run to different lengths: red reaches farthest, while blue and purple stop well short.

Francisco de Goya

Manuel Osorio Manrique de Zuñiga, 1787–88

Goya set one saturated red suit in a room of grays and browns, so the child is the strongest color in the picture.

Mixing in a gray of the same value dulls a color while keeping its lightness close to where it was.
