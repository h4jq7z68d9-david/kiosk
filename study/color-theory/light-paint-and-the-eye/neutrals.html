Light, Paint and the Eye

Neutrals

Neutrals are grays, browns and dull greens, and a painter can mix them from colors on opposite sides of the wheel.

Look at

  • The pure paints sit along the stepped edge; every other square mixes two of them.
  • Neighbors mix clean: chrome yellow with green gives a bright yellow-green.
  • Opposites mix dull: green with vermilion or crimson drops to brown and maroon.

Ville-d'Avray, 1870
