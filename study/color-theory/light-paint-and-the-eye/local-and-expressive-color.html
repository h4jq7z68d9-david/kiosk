Light, Paint and the Eye

Local and Expressive Color

Local color is the color a thing has in plain daylight; expressive color is chosen for feeling or design instead.

Look at

  • The left panel gives sky, hill and tree their everyday colors.
  • The middle panel keeps the forms but tints everything with sunset light, the way the eye sees it at that hour.
  • The right panel invents a green sky, pink hill and yellow tree that no daylight would give.

Paul Gauguin

Mahana no atua (Day of the God), 1894

Gauguin painted this in Paris from memory and fantasy, so the bank turns pink and the water breaks into flat zones of yellow, blue and violet.
