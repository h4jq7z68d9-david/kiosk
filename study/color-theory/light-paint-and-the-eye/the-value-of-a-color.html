Light, Paint and the Eye

The Value of a Color

Each hue reaches its full strength at a lightness of its own.

Look at

  • The center column is a gray scale running from black at the bottom to white at the top.
  • Lines tie each color to the gray of equal lightness, from yellow near the top to purple near the bottom.
  • Red and blue meet the same gray step, so a black-and-white photograph would turn them about the same gray.

James McNeill Whistler

Symphony in White, No. 1: The White Girl, 1861–1863, 1872

Whistler kept dress and curtain to one high, white value, so the red hair, the flowers and the blue carpet carry the few colors.
