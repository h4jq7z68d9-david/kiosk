Complementary Colors

Complementary colors sit directly across the wheel: red and green, orange and blue, yellow and violet. Side by side each makes the other look stronger; mixed as paint they dull each other.

Image: Wellcome Collection (UCL Library Services copy) via Internet Archive · Public domain

Van Gogh set blood-red walls against a green ceiling and billiard table; the opposites heighten each other into a clash he meant to feel harsh.
