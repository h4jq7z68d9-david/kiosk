Light, Paint and the Eye

Monochromatic

A monochromatic scheme uses a single hue, varied from light to dark.

Look at

  • One orange runs through a numbered scale, from 0, white, at the bottom to 21, black, at the top.
  • Each numbered step is only slightly darker than the one below it.
  • The middle steps carry the strongest orange; toward each end it pales or sinks.

James McNeill Whistler

Nocturne: Blue and Silver—Battersea Reach, 1870–75

Whistler washed river, sky and barges in thin layers of one gray-blue and let a few small lights break the single hue.
