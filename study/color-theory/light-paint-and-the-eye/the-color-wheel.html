Light, Paint and the Eye

The Color Wheel

A color wheel bends the hues into a circle, so neighbors and opposites can be read at a glance.

Look at

  • Narrow wedges step around the circle from red through orange, yellow, green, blue and violet, and back to red.
  • Each mixed hue sits between the two it comes from: orange between red and yellow, green between yellow and blue.

Paul Signac

Evening Calm, Concarneau, Opus 220 (Allegro Maestoso), 1891

Signac took his shadows from across the wheel: the hill catches yellow-orange evening light, and the rocks fall into violet-blue.

Painters build the wheel from red, yellow and blue, yet no three real paints can mix every hue on it.
