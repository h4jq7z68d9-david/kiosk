Light, Paint and the Eye

Optical Mixing

Small touches of two colors placed side by side blend in the eye into a third color without being mixed as paint.

Look at

  • Each band is printed as fine stripes of two colors laid side by side, never blended.
  • Red and yellow threads read as orange in the top band; red and blue read as a dark violet in the middle one.
  • Yellow threads scattered through blue turn the left end of the bottom band green.

Henri-Edmond Cross

Pines Along the Shore, 1896

Cross laid the shore in separate dabs of orange, pink and violet; from a step back they merge into warm, sunlit ground.

Chevreul had the Gobelins tapestry workshop weave such samples, and found that threads of opposite colors cancel toward gray instead.
