Light, Paint and the Eye

Triads

A triad is three colors spaced evenly around the wheel, such as red, yellow and blue, giving variety with balance.

Look at

  • Each disk is cut into three equal wedges, one color to a wedge.
  • Disk 9 holds the painter's red, yellow and blue; disk 10 a blue-green, an orange and a dark red-violet.
