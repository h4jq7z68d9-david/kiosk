Light, Paint and the Eye

Warm and Cool

Reds, oranges and yellows read as warm and blues, greens and violets as cool, though any color is warmer or cooler only beside another.

Look at

  • An axis crosses the circle with Hot marked near the orange side and Cold near the blue.
  • A second axis marks advancing and retiring colors, the old studio idea that warm colors come forward.

Joseph Mallord William Turner

The Fighting Temeraire, 1839

Turner splits the sky: a blazing orange sunset on the right, clear cool blue and a pale moon on the left.

Advancing warm color is only a tendency; on a light ground the effect can weaken or reverse.
