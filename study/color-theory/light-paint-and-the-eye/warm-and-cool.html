Warm and Cool

Reds, oranges, and yellows read as warm, like fire and sunlight, and blues, greens, and violets as cool, like water and shade, though any color is warmer or cooler only beside another.

Image: Getty Research Institute via Internet Archive · Public domain

Turner splits the sky between a blazing orange sunset on the right and cool blue on the left; each half looks warmer or cooler beside the other.
