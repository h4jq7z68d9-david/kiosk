Jan Toorop

Delftsche Slaolie

1894

Dutch, 1858–1928. Color lithograph, 37 1/2 × 24 1/2 in. Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

37 1/2 × 24 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

The two women's hair runs in long, parallel wavy lines that fill most of the background. Strong dark outlines keep the women themselves standing out against it.

  • Made for the Nederlandsche Oliefabriek, an oil maker in Delft, the poster became so well known that the Dutch nicknamed Art Nouveau the “salad oil style.”
