Théophile-Alexandre Steinlen

Tournée du Chat Noir

1896

Swiss-born, active in France, 1859–1923. Color lithograph, 55 × 39 in. Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

55 × 39 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

One flat black cat sits on a red band against bare paper, with a red halo behind its head. Almost nothing is shaded, so the cat reads as a single bold shape.

  • Le Chat Noir was a cabaret in Montmartre, Paris; the poster announced the coming tour of its performers with Rodolphe Salis.
