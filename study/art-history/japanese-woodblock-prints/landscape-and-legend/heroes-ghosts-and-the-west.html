Heroes, Ghosts and the West

Late Edo, Meiji, and prints abroad, 1827–1927

In the 1800s battle scenes, ghosts and foreign newcomers grew into major subjects, even as censors banned actor prints for several years. Abroad, Western painters and printmakers borrowed the prints' ways of composing, and by the 1920s Yoshida Hiroshi had carried the woodblock into modern times.
