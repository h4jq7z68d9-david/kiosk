Landscape and Legend

Utagawa Kuniyoshi

Hu Sanniang, from One Hundred and Eight Heroes of the Popular Water Margin

c. 1827/30

Color woodblock print, 39.5 × 27 cm. The Art Institute of Chicago

A woman warrior of the Chinese outlaw novel braces amid flying arrows, a sword in each hand.

  • Sarah Thompson (MFA Boston) calls this series his breakthrough, launching the warrior print.
  • Armor, robes and foliage crowd the sheet in dense, swirling pattern.
  • She adds that Chinese novels were a fairly safe subject under the censors.
