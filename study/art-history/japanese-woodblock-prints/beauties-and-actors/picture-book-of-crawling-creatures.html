Beauties and Actors

Kitagawa Utamaro

Picture Book of Crawling Creatures

1788

Page from a woodblock-printed book; ink and color on paper, 26.7 × 18.4 cm. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

A tree cricket clings to a seed head by comic verses; a firefly climbs the grasses opposite.

  • Insects and other small creatures fill all fifteen designs.
  • The verses were chosen, and the preface written, by the poet-scholar Yadoya no Meshimori.
  • Picture books like this paired prints with verse by comic-poetry (kyōka) circles.
