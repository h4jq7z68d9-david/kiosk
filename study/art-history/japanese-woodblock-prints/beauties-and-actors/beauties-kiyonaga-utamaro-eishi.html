Beauties: Kiyonaga, Utamaro, Eishi

From full figures to close-ups, 1780s–90s

The last quarter of the 1700s is often called the golden age of the print. Rival designers each offered an ideal of feminine beauty, and new formats such as the bust portrait followed.

  • A shifting ideal of the beautiful woman
  • Poetry circles, publishers and picture books
  • Outings, pastimes and private moments
