Stars of the Stage: Actor Prints

How fans followed their stars on paper, c. 1715–1824

Women were banned from the kabuki stage in 1629 and boys in 1652, leaving it to adult men. Fans bought pictures of favorite stars and the roles they made famous.

  • Fan culture and the business of star images
  • How designers showed who was on stage
