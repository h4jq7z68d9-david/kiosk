Japan, 1680s–1880s

Chōbunsai Eishi

Ono no Komachi Visiting Kiyomizu Temple

about 1788

Japanese, 1756–1829. Color woodblock print, 15 1/4 × 10 in. The Art Institute of Chicago

Eishi kept the colors few and pale (pink, yellow, olive green and gray) and used black only in sharp patches.

  • Black on the hair and a sash is the only strong tone, so the colors around it look paler.
  • Cherry blossom is pale pink with no black outline, so it nearly fades into the paper and the dark leaves look sharper.
  • Two women and a boy view cherry blossoms at a temple in Kyoto; the boy fastens a slip of paper with a poem to a branch.
