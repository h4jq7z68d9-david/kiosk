Japan, 1680s–1880s

Suzuki Harunobu

Woman Admiring Plum Blossoms at Night

about 1766

Woodblock print with embossing; ink and color on paper, 12 3/4 × 8 1/4 in. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Suzuki Harunobu (Japanese, 1725–1770). A solid black night covers about half the sheet.

Look at

  • White blossoms and the lantern stand out sharply against the dark.
  • The robe is a flat pale-orange shape covered in thin stripes and grey roundels, with no shading.
  • The orange railing crosses the sheet on a diagonal and is cut off at both sides.
  • The figure is slight, with a small round face and tiny hands.
  • A straight-edged band of cloud closes the top.
  • Full-color printing, with a separate carved block for each color, began in Edo in 1765. The socks and inner robes are embossed: pressed into the paper without ink.
