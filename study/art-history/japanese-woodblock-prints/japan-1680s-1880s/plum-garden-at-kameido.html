Japan, 1680s–1880s

Utagawa Hiroshige

Plum Garden at Kameido

1857

Color woodblock print, 14 1/4 × 9 1/2 in. The Art Institute of Chicago

A dark tree trunk is pushed right up against the viewer and cropped by the edges of the sheet.

Look at

  • Trunk and branches are flat dark shapes that cross the whole picture.
  • The sky is red and fades to white at the horizon; the ground is a flat green.
  • Between the branches, the far trees and visitors are tiny.
  • Thin straight shoots rise from the branches to the top edge.
  • Blossoms are white disks set on the red and the green.
  • From One Hundred Famous Views of Edo. Vincent van Gogh painted a copy of the design in 1887.
