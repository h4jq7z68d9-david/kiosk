Katsushika Hokusai

Under the Wave off Kanagawa (The Great Wave)

about 1830–32

Japanese, 1760–1849. Woodblock print; ink and color on paper, 10 × 15 in. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

10 × 15 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Hokusai set a huge near wave against a tiny far mountain and printed the wave in three blues.

  • From the series Thirty-six Views of Mount Fuji. The artist was known for landscapes printed with indigo and Prussian blue, an imported pigment.
