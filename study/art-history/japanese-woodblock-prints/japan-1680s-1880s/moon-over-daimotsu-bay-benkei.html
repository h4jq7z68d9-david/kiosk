Japan, 1680s–1880s

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi

Moon over Daimotsu Bay: Benkei

1886

Woodblock print; ink and color on paper, 13 3/4 × 9 3/4 in. Minneapolis Institute of Art

Tsukioka Yoshitoshi (Japanese, 1839–1892). A tall black wave curls up the left side, and its foam reaches across the moon.

Look at

  • Black and grey cover almost the whole sheet.
  • Foam is drawn in thin lines that fray into wisps.
  • Cloud and water are layered in several greys, from pale to near black.
  • The moon is a pale disk half hidden by cloud and foam, the brightest shape in the sky.
  • Bright red marks one cord at the figure's chest and the seal at lower left.
  • The warrior-monk Benkei prays in a storm so that slain enemies will not rise from the water. From the series One Hundred Aspects of the Moon.
