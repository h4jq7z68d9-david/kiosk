Japan, 1680s–1880s

Utagawa Hiroshige

Fukagawa Susaki and Jūmantsubo

1857

Color woodblock print, 13 × 8 3/4 in. The Art Institute of Chicago

The view is from high in the air, with an eagle hanging across the top of the sheet.

Look at

  • The eagle is cut off by the top edge, with wings spread across the full width.
  • The land below is a pattern of small marks for trees and huts.
  • The water darkens to deep blue at the bottom of the sheet.
  • Snow on the ground is unprinted, and white specks in the sky are falling flakes.
  • Feathers are built from rows of small scalloped lines in grey and brown.
  • Edo Bay under snow. Viewers at the time could only imagine such a view from the air.
