Japan, 1680s–1880s

Okumura Masanobu

Perspective View of the Interior of the Nakamura Theater

1740

Hand-colored woodblock print, 15 3/4 × 25 in. The Cleveland Museum of Art

Okumura Masanobu (Japanese, 1686–1764). Ceiling boards and side galleries slant toward one point behind the stage, and the rows of lanterns shrink with distance.

Look at

  • Heads in the crowd shrink row by row toward the stage.
  • The two side galleries narrow like the walls of a box, so the hall reads as deep space.
  • Hand-brushed yellow, red and red-brown are the only colors.
  • The roofed stage sits small at the center, under three rows of lanterns.
  • A perspective print of a kabuki theater. The designer was probably the earliest printmaker to use perspective.
