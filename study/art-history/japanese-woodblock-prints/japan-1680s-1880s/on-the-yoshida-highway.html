Japan, 1680s–1880s

Sugimura Jihei (attributed)

On the Yoshida Highway

about 1685

Japanese, active about 1681–98. Hand-colored woodblock print, 23 1/4 × 11 1/2 in. The Art Institute of Chicago

The print itself is black only; the color was brushed on by hand afterward.

  • Orange-red and dull green strokes run loosely over collars and hems without filling the outlines, so they read as brushwork over the print.
  • Checks and crests printed in black cover most of each robe, so the picture looks complete even without the color.
  • A hand-colored print of this type is called a tan-e; before 1765 printmakers worked in one color and painted the rest in by hand. The labels name the characters, likely from an early play.
