Japan, 1680s–1880s

Sugimura Jihei (attributed)

On the Yoshida Highway

about 1685

Hand-colored woodblock print, 23 1/4 × 11 1/2 in. The Art Institute of Chicago

Attributed to Sugimura Jihei (Japanese, active about 1681–98). The print itself is black line; the color was brushed on by hand afterward.

Look at

  • Strokes of orange-red and dull green run loosely over collars, sashes and hems and do not fill the outlines.
  • Most of each robe is pattern printed in black: checks, crests, clouds and leaves.
  • Four large figures fill the tall sheet in two tiers, with little empty ground.
  • Beams and a floor edge slope up to the right in the background.
  • The hand-colored print is a type called tan-e. The labels name the characters, likely from an early play.
