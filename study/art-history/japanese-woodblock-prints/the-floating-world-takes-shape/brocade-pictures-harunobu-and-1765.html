Brocade Pictures: Harunobu and 1765

A decade of slender figures and soft color, 1765–1770s

In 1765 Edo printers began printing each color from its own carved block, one after another, so a sheet could carry many hues. Wealthy patrons first ordered them as New Year calendars, and the new prints became known as brocade pictures (nishiki-e).

  • Calendars, private commissions and the first full-color sheets
  • A delicate, youthful ideal of beauty
  • Old poems and legends in modern dress
