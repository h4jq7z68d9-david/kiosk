Ink Line to Hand Color

Pictures for Edo's townspeople, c. 1680–1764

Single-sheet prints grew out of Edo's illustrated books in the late 1600s and found buyers among the city's townspeople. For most of this period color was added by hand; printed color came only gradually.

  • Pictures cheap enough for city buyers to own
  • Courtesans and kabuki stars as favorite subjects
  • Named designers and the first family studios
