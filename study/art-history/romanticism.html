Romanticism

Feeling, imagination and wild nature

Romanticism is the name given to a shift in art and literature that was at its height from about 1780 to 1830 and was felt long after. Artists in Britain, Spain, Germany, France and the United States turned away from classical restraint and put personal feeling, the workings of the mind and the natural world at the center of their pictures.
