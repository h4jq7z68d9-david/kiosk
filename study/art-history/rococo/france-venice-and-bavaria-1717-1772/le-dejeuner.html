France, Venice and Bavaria, 1717–1772

François Boucher

Le Déjeuner

1739

Oil on canvas, 32 × 25 3/4 in. Musée du Louvre, Paris

A Rococo interior of curling gilt frames, clock and console, with a breakfast going on inside it.

Look at

  • The clock case, the candle brackets, the mirror crest and the gilt table at the far left are all C- and S-curves.
  • Straight lines come only from the window bars, the mirror rails and the floorboards, and they set off the curls.
  • A tall window at the left lights the group; the mirror reflects a doorway, which makes the shallow room read as deep.
  • Colour comes in small saturated patches, a red lacquer table, a red cape and a blue dress, against gold and brown.
  • A Chinese figure sits on the shelf behind the man: the taste for chinoiserie was part of the style.
