Dominikus and Johann Baptist Zimmermann

Pilgrimage Church of Wies, interior

1745–54

Stucco, fresco and gilding. Steingaden, Bavaria

Dominikus Zimmermann (German, 1685–1766) and Johann Baptist Zimmermann (German, 1680–1758). The same style built at full scale: white and gold stucco curling under a painted ceiling.

Look at

  • The walls and piers are white, and the gold ornament runs over them in scrolls that break across every straight edge.
  • The pulpit and the capitals are piled with stucco foliage and figures, so the architecture seems to grow rather than to be built.
  • Tall clear windows light the nave, and the white walls pick up the daylight.
  • The chancel narrows between red-marbled columns, and the colour deepens from the white nave toward the gilt altar.
  • The ceiling frescoes sit inside curling white frames, and the painted sky seems to open above the stucco.
  • A pilgrimage church in the Bavarian countryside, designed by one brother and decorated by the other.
