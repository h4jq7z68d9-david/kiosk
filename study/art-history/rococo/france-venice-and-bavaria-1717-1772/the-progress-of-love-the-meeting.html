Jean-Honoré Fragonard

The Progress of Love: The Meeting

1771–72

Oil on canvas, 125 × 96 in. The Frick Collection, New York

A tall wall panel in which the garden takes most of the height and the figures are small at the bottom.

Look at

  • The two figures fill only the bottom third; above them a statue, an urn and a mass of trees rise to the top of the panel.
  • The trees are a dome of short green touches lit along their edges, against a sky blended smooth.
  • A white dress at the left and a red coat at the right pull against each other across the grey pedestal.
  • Roses tumble over the stone at both lower corners, loose and quickly painted.
  • One of a set of garden scenes painted for Madame du Barry's pavilion outside Paris, then returned to the painter.
