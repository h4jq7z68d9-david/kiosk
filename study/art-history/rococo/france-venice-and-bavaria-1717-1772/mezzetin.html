France, Venice and Bavaria, 1717–1772

Antoine Watteau

Mezzetin

c. 1718–20

Oil on canvas, 21 3/4 × 17 in. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

A small canvas built on one serpentine curve and a shower of broken strokes.

Look at

  • The body twists in a spiral: feet and knees pushed to the lower left, guitar across the lap, head thrown back to the upper right, one long S against the straight bench.
  • The pink, white and pale blue stripes of the costume are laid in with a small quick brush, each stroke left separate.
  • The pink of the cap, cape and rosettes is the strongest colour, set against dark green foliage.
  • A grey statue in the garden turns her back on the singer, a small joke placed in the shadow.
  • A comic character from the Italian stage, singing a serenade.
