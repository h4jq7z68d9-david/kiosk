Antoine Watteau

Pilgrimage to the Island of Cythera

1717

Oil on canvas, 50 3/4 × 76 1/2 in. Musée du Louvre, Paris

Antoine Watteau (French, 1684–1721). The air comes first: a hillside of couples dissolving into a hazy pink and blue distance.

Look at

  • The far shore, the lake and the mountains are a soft blue-grey haze, with no hard edge anywhere in the distance.
  • The trees are loose clusters of small touches, thin enough for the warm ground to show through.
  • The couples run in a chain from the statue at the right down to the boat at the left, so the eye drifts across the hill like a slow walk.
  • The silks are where the paint thickens, in small bright touches of rose, gold and white.
  • Delivered to the Paris Academy in 1717 as a reception piece and registered as a fête galante, an elegant party out of doors.
