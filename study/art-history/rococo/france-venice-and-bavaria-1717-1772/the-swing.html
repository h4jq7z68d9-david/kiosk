Jean-Honoré Fragonard

The Swing

c. 1767–68

Oil on canvas, 32 × 25 1/4 in. The Wallace Collection, London

Jean-Honoré Fragonard (French, 1732–1806). One shaft of sunlight falls through the trees onto a pink dress.

Look at

  • The pink dress and the patch of pale sky at the upper left are the two brightest passages; the rest of the garden sinks into blue-green shadow.
  • The foliage is painted in loose, feathery touches, and the leaves at the edges dissolve into the light.
  • The ropes run down from the upper right to the seat, and the kicked leg and flying slipper carry the movement on to the left.
  • The dress is the most tightly worked part, its folds caught in small crisp strokes of white and pink.
  • A young woman on a swing, a man hidden in the bushes below, and a stone cupid with a finger to his lips.
