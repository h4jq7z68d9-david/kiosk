François Boucher

The Triumph of Venus

1740

Oil on canvas, 51 1/4 × 63 3/4 in. Nationalmuseum, Stockholm

François Boucher (French, 1703–1770). The composition is one great spiral of pink flesh, striped silk and blue-green sea.

Look at

  • A striped pink-and-white cloth loops across the top of the canvas and a chain of bodies curves around below, so nothing in the picture comes to rest.
  • The colour is held to blue and pink: blue sky, blue drapery and sea against pink skin and rose cloth.
  • The flesh is smooth and pearly, with blended edges, against water painted in short curling strokes.
  • The dark cloud and cliff at the upper right are the one heavy mass; the cloud's edge melts into the pale sky while the rock stays sharp.
  • Venus carried over the waves by sea nymphs and tritons; shown at the Paris Salon of 1740.
