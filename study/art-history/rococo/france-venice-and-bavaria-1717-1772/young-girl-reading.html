Jean-Honoré Fragonard

Young Girl Reading

c. 1769

Oil on canvas, 32 × 25 1/2 in. National Gallery of Art, Washington

Speed: a picture put down fast in lemon yellow and left showing.

Look at

  • Long loaded strokes run down the sleeve, across the bodice and over the cushion, none of them blended.
  • The shadows inside the white ruff are scratched into wet paint, drawn with the brush handle rather than painted.
  • A single yellow carries the picture, against a grey-brown wall and a lilac-pink cushion.
  • The profile and the hand are worked to a finish; everything else is left as sketch.
  • One of the fantasy figures, a set of quickly painted half-length figures in fancy dress.
