Antoine Watteau

Pierrot

c. 1718–19

Oil on canvas, 72 1/2 × 61 1/4 in. Musée du Louvre, Paris

White on white: a whole costume painted in thin, shimmering layers of satin.

Look at

  • The costume is many whites at once, cool blue-grey in the folds and warm cream on the sleeves, so the cloth reads as satin.
  • The figure stands as one pale column from collar to shoes, hands hanging, in front of a sky of patchy blue and cream.
  • The other actors are pushed down into a strip of heads at knee height, which leaves the white figure standing alone.
  • The brushwork in the hat, ruff and ribbons is quick and loose, with the edges left soft.
  • A stock character from the Italian comedy.
