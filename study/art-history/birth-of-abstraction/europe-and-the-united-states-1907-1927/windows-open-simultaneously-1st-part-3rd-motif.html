Robert Delaunay

Windows Open Simultaneously 1st Part, 3rd Motif

1912

French, 1885–1941. Oil on canvas, about 22 1/2 × 48 1/2 in. Peggy Guggenheim Collection, Venice

about 22 1/2 × 48 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Planes of clear color overlap across the oval canvas, and changes of color, not drawn lines, separate one plane from the next. A green Eiffel Tower, reduced to a dark spire, still stands at the center.

  • In the spring of 1912 Delaunay began the Windows series, which carried him toward painting with no recognizable subject.
