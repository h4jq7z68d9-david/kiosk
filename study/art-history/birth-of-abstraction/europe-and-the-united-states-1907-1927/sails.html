Arthur Dove

Sails

1911–12

American, 1880–1946. Pastel on composition board mounted on wood panel, 17 3/4 × 21 1/2 in. Terra Foundation for American Art, Chicago

17 3/4 × 21 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Tall, pointed tan shapes curve up across the picture like sails against dark green and blue, but the boats and the water are never clearly drawn.

  • One of ten pastels later called the Ten Commandments, it was shown at Alfred Stieglitz's gallery 291 in New York in 1912 and then in Chicago.
