Jacopo Tintoretto

The Origin of the Milky Way

about 1575

Italian, about 1518–1594. Oil on canvas, 58 3/4 × 66 1/4 in. The National Gallery, London

58 3/4 × 66 1/4 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Jupiter dives in from the upper right to set his infant son Hercules at the breast of the sleeping Juno; her spilled milk became the Milky Way. Every body tilts or tumbles, and none stands upright.

  • Some time before 1727, roughly the lower third of the canvas was cut away. An old, smaller copy shows the missing part.
