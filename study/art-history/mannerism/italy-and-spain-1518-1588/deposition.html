Jacopo Pontormo

Deposition

1525–28

Paint on wood panel, 123 1/4 × 75 1/2 in. Capponi Chapel, Santa Felicita, Florence

123 1/4 × 75 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Most of the colors are mixed with white, so the pinks, blues, and greens look pale and the bodies seem to weigh little. There is no cross and no landscape, only a single cloud.

  • Made for the banker Ludovico Capponi's family chapel, where it still hangs. Pontormo let no one, not even Capponi, see it until it was unveiled.
