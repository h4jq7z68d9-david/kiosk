Parmigianino

Madonna with the Long Neck

1534–40

Oil on panel, 85 1/4 × 52 1/4 in. The Uffizi, Florence

85 1/4 × 52 1/4 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Mary's neck, fingers, and body are drawn out far past life, and the tall white column beside her repeats the length of her neck.

  • Commissioned in 1534 for a church in Parma, it was still unfinished in Parmigianino's studio when he died in 1540. The Latin line at the column's base, added two years later, says fate kept him from completing it.
