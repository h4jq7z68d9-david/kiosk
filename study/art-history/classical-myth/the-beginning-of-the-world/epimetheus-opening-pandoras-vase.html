The Beginning of the World · The Origins of Mortals

Giulio Bonasone

Epimetheus Opening Pandora’s Vase

mid-16th century

Engraving · Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Made for: A print sold to collectors and artists; prints like this spread mythological imagery across Europe.

To punish humans for the stolen fire, Zeus had Hephaestus shape the first woman from earth and water; Athena taught her weaving, Aphrodite gave her desire, and Hermes gave her a deceitful mind. She was called Pandora, “all gifts,” and was presented to Epimetheus. Bonasone’s print is unusual in showing Epimetheus himself lifting the lid as the evils fly out.

  • Pandora made by Hephaestus, gifted by the gods
  • Presented to Epimetheus
  • Here Epimetheus opens the vessel
