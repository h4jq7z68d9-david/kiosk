The Beginning of the World · The Origins of Mortals

Michelangelo

The Deluge

1508–09

Fresco · Sistine Chapel ceiling, Vatican

Made for: One of the nine Genesis scenes on the ceiling of the pope’s chapel, commissioned by Julius II.

Michelangelo shows desperate families climbing to high ground while Noah’s ark floats in the distance. Placed among Greek myths, the scene shows how widely the flood story spread. Each culture gives it a different reason: overpopulation in Mesopotamia, human wickedness in Genesis and in the Greek version.

  • Refugees in the foreground, ark behind
