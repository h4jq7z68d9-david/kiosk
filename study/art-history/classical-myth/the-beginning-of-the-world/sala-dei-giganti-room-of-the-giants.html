Giulio Romano

Sala dei Giganti (Room of the Giants)

1532–35

Fresco · Palazzo Te, Mantua

Made for: A room in the pleasure palace of Federico II Gonzaga, Duke of Mantua, painted on every wall and the ceiling to overwhelm visitors.

Giulio Romano painted the Giants crushed under collapsing columns and mountains as Jupiter hurls thunderbolts from the dome above. Because the painting covers the whole room, visitors feel the building falling around them. The theme flattered Federico’s patron, the Emperor Charles V, as a Jupiter who crushes rebels.

  • Whole room painted floor to ceiling
  • Jupiter crushes the Giants
  • Political flattery of Charles V

Themes

  • Order defeats chaos
