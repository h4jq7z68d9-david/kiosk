Peter Paul Rubens

The Judgement of Paris

probably 1632–35

Flemish, 1577–1640. Oil on panel, probably oak, 57 × 76 1/4 in. The National Gallery, London

57 × 76 1/4 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

The shepherd Paris must give a golden apple to the most beautiful of three goddesses. Rubens set their pale, warm bodies against dark trees and showed each from a different side: front, side, and back.

  • Paris hands the apple to Venus, in the middle; Minerva's owl sits in the tree at left, and Juno's peacock stands at her feet. The National Gallery bought it in 1844.
