Gods and stories

Love, Desire and Transformation

The loves of the gods, and bodies that change shape

Many myths turn on desire: gods pursue mortals, often in disguise, and the chase ends in a change of form. Ovid gathered these stories in the Metamorphoses, and from the Renaissance on they gave painters a respectable reason to paint the nude.

  • Titian’s poesie for Philip II set the standard for painting these stories.
  • Gods transform themselves to seduce; mortals are transformed to escape or as punishment.
  • Many of these paintings were made for private rooms and bedchambers.
Metamorphosis
A change of shape, the theme of Ovid’s poem of about 8 CE and the main source for these paintings.
Poesia
Titian’s word for his mythological paintings for Philip II: painted poems based on Ovid.
