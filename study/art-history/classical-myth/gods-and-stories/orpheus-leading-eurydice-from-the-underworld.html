Camille Corot

Orpheus Leading Eurydice from the Underworld

1861

Oil on canvas, 112.7 × 137.2 cm · Museum of Fine Arts, Houston

Shown at the Paris Salon of 1861, this is the painter’s soft, misty landscape style applied to myth. The mood foreshadows the ending rather than showing it.

  • Spirits of the dead gather beneath feathery trees.
  • A narrow range of grays, greens and blues gives a filtered, dreamlike light.
  • Signed “COROT” at lower left; bought by the museum in 1987.
