Wine, Harvest and the Underworld

Demeter, Dionysus and the journey to the land of the dead

Two gods of the earth’s cycles, Demeter of the grain and Dionysus of the vine, stand close to the world of the dead. Their myths explain the seasons and promise renewal, while stories of journeys to the underworld test what happens after death.

  • Persephone’s yearly return from the underworld explains spring.
  • Dionysus brings wine, ecstasy and theatre, and punishes those who refuse him.
  • Heroes and poets who visit the underworld bring back warnings for the living.
