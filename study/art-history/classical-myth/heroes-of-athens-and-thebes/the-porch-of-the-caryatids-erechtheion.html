Carl Friedrich Heinrich Werner

The Porch of the Caryatids, Erechtheion

1877

Watercolor · Benaki Museum, Athens · view of the temple on the Athenian Acropolis (built 421–406 BCE)

Made for: A travel watercolor for collectors; the temple itself sheltered Athens’ oldest cults, including the tomb of Cecrops.

The Erechtheion on the Acropolis held the sacred spots of Athens’ origin myths: the mark of Poseidon’s trident, Athena’s olive tree, and the tomb of Cecrops. Athena gave the infant Erichthonius in a closed basket to Cecrops’ daughters and forbade them to look; two did, saw the snake-child, and leapt from the Acropolis in madness. Each year young girls called Arrhephoroi carried secret objects in baskets for Athena, recalling the story.
