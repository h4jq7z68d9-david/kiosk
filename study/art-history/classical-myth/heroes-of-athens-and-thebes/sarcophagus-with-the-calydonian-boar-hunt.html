Sarcophagus with the Calydonian Boar Hunt

Roman, 2nd–3rd century CE

Marble · Capitoline Museums, Rome

Made for: A coffin for a wealthy Roman; hunting myths celebrated the courage of the dead.

Artemis sent a monstrous boar to ravage Calydon, and Prince Meleager gathered heroes, among them the huntress Atalanta, to kill it. Meleager gave her the prize because she drew first blood, and in the quarrel that followed he killed his uncles. His mother, Althaea, then burned the log on which his life depended, and he died. The sarcophagus packs hunters, dogs, and the boar into one crowded frieze.
