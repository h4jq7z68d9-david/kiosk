Heroes of Athens and Thebes · Theseus and Crete

John William Waterhouse

Ariadne

1898

Oil on canvas · private collection

Made for: Shown at the Royal Academy in London; Waterhouse painted many sleeping or dreaming women from classical myth.

On the way home Theseus abandoned Ariadne asleep on the island of Naxos, where Dionysus found and married her. Waterhouse shows her asleep by the sea while Dionysus’s leopards approach and Theseus’s ship sails off in the distance. Theseus forgot to change his black sail to white, the sign of success, and his father Aegeus, seeing it, threw himself into the sea, now called the Aegean.
