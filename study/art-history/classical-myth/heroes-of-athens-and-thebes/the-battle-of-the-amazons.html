Heroes of Athens and Thebes · Theseus and Crete

Peter Paul Rubens

The Battle of the Amazons

ca. 1615–18

Oil on panel · Alte Pinakothek, Munich

Made for: Painted for the Antwerp merchant and collector Cornelis van der Geest; it was soon famous through engravings.

Theseus joined Heracles against the Amazons, warrior women from the edge of the world, and carried off their queen Antiope (sometimes called Hippolyta). The Amazons then invaded Athens and were defeated. Rubens packs the battle onto a bridge, with horses and riders tumbling into the river below. Greek artists loved the Amazonomachy as a battle of Greek men against a reversed world where women fight.

  • Theseus abducts the Amazon queen
  • Amazons invade and are beaten
  • Greeks vs. the “other”
Amazonomachy
