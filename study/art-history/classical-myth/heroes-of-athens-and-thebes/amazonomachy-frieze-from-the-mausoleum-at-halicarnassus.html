Heroes of Athens and Thebes · Theseus and Crete

Amazonomachy frieze from the Mausoleum at Halicarnassus

ca. 350 BCE

Marble · British Museum, London

Made for: Part of the sculptural decoration of the huge tomb of Mausolus, ruler of Caria, one of the Seven Wonders of the ancient world.

Theseus joined Heracles against the Amazons, warrior women from the edge of the world, and carried off their queen Antiope (sometimes called Hippolyta). The Amazons then invaded Athens and were defeated. Their son was Hippolytus. Greek artists loved the Amazonomachy as a battle of Greek men against a reversed world where women fight.

  • Theseus abducts the Amazon queen
  • Amazons invade and are beaten
  • Greeks vs. the “other”
Amazonomachy
The battle between Greeks and Amazons, a favorite subject of temple and tomb sculpture.
Antiope (Hippolyta)
Amazon queen taken by Theseus; mother of Hippolytus.
