Aurora

1613–14

Fresco · Casino dell’Aurora Pallavicini, Rome

Painted on the ceiling of a garden pavilion built in 1612–13 for Cardinal Scipione Borghese as a summer retreat. It is still in private hands and opens to visitors on the first day of most months.

  • Veiled Aurora, the dawn, flies ahead scattering flowers.
  • Apollo drives the sun’s four-horse chariot, ringed by the Hours.
  • Phosphorus, the morning star, flies among the figures.
  • Most recently restored and unveiled in 2022.

Each morning Aurora opens the gates of heaven for the sun. The daily return of light became an image of joy and renewal.
