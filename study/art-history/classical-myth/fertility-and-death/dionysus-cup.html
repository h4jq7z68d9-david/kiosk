Exekias

Dionysus Cup

Athenian, ca. 530 BCE

Black-figure kylix (interior) · Staatliche Antikensammlungen, Munich

Made for: A drinking cup for the symposium; as the wine was drunk, the god appeared sailing on a wine-colored sea.

In a Homeric Hymn, pirates kidnap Dionysus, thinking he is a prince. Vines sprout from the mast, wine floods the ship, and the god becomes a lion; the terrified sailors leap overboard and turn into dolphins. Exekias, the greatest black-figure painter, fills the whole interior with coral-red slip so the scene floats on a glowing sea.

  • Pirates kidnap Dionysus
  • Vines from the mast, sailors become dolphins
  • Coral-red interior turns the cup into a sea

“And all at once a vine spread out both ways along the top of the sail with many clusters hanging down from it”

Homeric Hymn to Dionysus 38–40, trans. Evelyn-White
