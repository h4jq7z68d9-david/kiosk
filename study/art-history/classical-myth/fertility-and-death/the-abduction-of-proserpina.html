Rembrandt

The Abduction of Proserpina

ca. 1631

Oil on panel · Gemäldegalerie, Berlin

Made for: Painted in Leiden early in Rembrandt’s career; it was soon in the collection of the Dutch stadholder Frederik Hendrik.

Hades seized Persephone while she gathered flowers and carried her off in his chariot to the underworld, with Zeus’s permission. Rembrandt makes it a struggle: she claws at Hades’s face while her companions cling to her dress, and the chariot plunges toward dark water. The painting is small, but the violence of the moment fills it.
