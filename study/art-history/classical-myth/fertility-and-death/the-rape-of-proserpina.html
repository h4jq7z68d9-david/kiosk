Fertility and Death · Demeter and Dionysus

Gian Lorenzo Bernini

The Rape of Proserpina

1621–22

Marble · Galleria Borghese, Rome

Made for: Carved for Cardinal Scipione Borghese, who gave it to Cardinal Ludovisi; it returned to the Borghese villa in 1908.

Hades seized Persephone while she gathered flowers and carried her off in his chariot to the underworld, with Zeus’s permission. The sculptor, only in his early twenties, makes hard marble look soft: Hades’s fingers press into her thigh as she twists away, tears on her cheek. The three-headed Cerberus guards the base.

  • Hades abducts Persephone
  • Marble made to look like flesh

Abduction of Persephone
Hades carries off Demeter’s daughter; her return for part of each year explains the seasons.
