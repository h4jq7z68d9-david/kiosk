Jan Brueghel the Younger

Aeneas and the Sibyl in the Underworld

1630s

Oil on copper · Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Made for: A small, jewel-like painting on copper, a support often used for collectors’ cabinet pictures.

In Book 6 of Virgil’s Aeneid, the Cumaean Sibyl leads Aeneas down through a cave near Lake Avernus to visit his dead father, Anchises. They cross the Acheron in Charon’s boat and pass the places of punishment before reaching Elysium, where Anchises shows his son the souls of future Romans. Brueghel crowds the dark landscape with fires, monsters, and tiny tormented figures.

  • Aeneid 6: descent at Lake Avernus
  • Guided by the Cumaean Sibyl
  • Anchises shows Rome’s future
Lake Avernus
Volcanic lake near Naples believed to be an entrance to the underworld.
Cumaean Sibyl
Prophetess of Apollo at Cumae who guides Aeneas.
Anchises
Aeneas’s father, met in Elysium.
