Myth and Its World · The Nature of Myth

Titian

Bacchus and Ariadne

1520–23

Oil on canvas · National Gallery, London

Made for: Painted for the private study of Alfonso d’Este, Duke of Ferrara, as part of a series of mythological scenes.

Dionysus, the Roman Bacchus, is the god of wine, ecstasy, and theater, and the only Olympian with a mortal mother, the Theban princess Semele. Titian shows him leaping from his leopard-drawn chariot toward Ariadne, the Cretan princess abandoned by Theseus, whom he marries and later places in the sky as a constellation. His noisy followers, the satyrs and maenads, fill the right side.

  • Wine, revelry, theater
  • Attributes: grapevine, ivy, wine cup, leopard
  • Mother Semele was mortal
