Mattei Athena

Roman copy of a Greek bronze of the mid-4th century BCE

Marble · Musée du Louvre; on view at Louvre Abu Dhabi

Made for: A Roman copy of a Greek cult-type statue; wealthy Romans collected copies of famous Greek sculpture for villas and public buildings.

Athena, the Roman Minerva, is the virgin goddess of wisdom, crafts such as weaving, and the planning side of war. She was born fully armed from Zeus’s head after he swallowed her mother, the Titaness Metis (“cleverness”). She wears a helmet and the aegis, a goatskin cape bearing the Gorgon’s head. Athens, named for her, saw her as its protector.

  • Wisdom, crafts, strategic war
  • Attributes: helmet, spear, aegis with Gorgon head, owl, olive
  • Patron goddess of Athens
