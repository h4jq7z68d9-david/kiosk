Ludovisi Ares

Roman copy of a Greek original of ca. 320 BCE

Marble, restored by Bernini in 1622 · Palazzo Altemps, Rome

Made for: A Roman decorative statue later restored and displayed in the Ludovisi family collection.

Ares, the Roman Mars, is the god of war in its violent, bloody form, unlike Athena, who stands for planning and strategy. The Greeks disliked him, but the Romans honored Mars as the father of Romulus, and the month of March is named for him. Here he sits at rest with his shield, and a small Eros at his feet hints at his love affair with Aphrodite.

  • Violent, physical side of war
  • Attributes: helmet, spear, shield
  • Lover of Aphrodite; honored by Rome as Mars
