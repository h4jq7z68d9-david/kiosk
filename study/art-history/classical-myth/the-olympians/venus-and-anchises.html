Venus and Anchises

print after a fresco of 1597–1608

Engraving · Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam; fresco in the Palazzo Farnese, Rome

Made for: A print reproducing one scene of Carracci’s ceiling for Cardinal Odoardo Farnese; prints like this spread famous frescoes across Europe.

Zeus made Aphrodite herself fall for a mortal, the Trojan shepherd-prince Anchises. As the Homeric Hymn to Aphrodite tells, she visited him disguised as a girl and later revealed herself, warning him never to boast of it. Their son was Aeneas, the hero who would escape Troy and found the Roman people. Carracci’s fresco shows Anchises removing her sandal.
