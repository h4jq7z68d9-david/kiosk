Titian

Diana and Actaeon

1556–59

Oil on canvas · National Gallery, London, and National Galleries of Scotland, Edinburgh

Made for: One of the poesie for Philip II of Spain, now owned jointly by the two museums.

The hunter Actaeon stumbled on Artemis bathing with her nymphs. Furious that a man had seen her naked, she turned him into a stag, and his own hounds tore him apart. Titian shows the instant of discovery: Actaeon pulls back a curtain as the goddess snatches a cloth and glares. The stag skull on the column predicts his fate.

  • Actaeon sees Artemis bathing
  • Turned into a stag, killed by his dogs
  • Stag skull foretells his fate
