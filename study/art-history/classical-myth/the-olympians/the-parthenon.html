The Parthenon

447–432 BCE

Marble · Acropolis, Athens

Made for: A temple to Athena Parthenos (“the maiden”) that also served as the treasury of Athens and its empire.

Athens built the Parthenon after the Persian Wars, on the Acropolis the Persians had burned. Its sculptures showed Athena’s birth, her contest with Poseidon for Athens, and battles of order against chaos. Inside stood Phidias’s colossal gold-and-ivory statue of the goddess. The building became the model of Classical architecture for more than two thousand years.
