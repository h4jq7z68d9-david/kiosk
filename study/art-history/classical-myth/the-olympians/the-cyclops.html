The Olympians · Zeus, Hera, Poseidon, and Apollo

Odilon Redon

The Cyclops

ca. 1914

Oil on cardboard mounted on panel · Kröller-Müller Museum, Otterlo

Made for: A late Symbolist painting, made for collectors of Redon’s dreamlike work.

Polyphemus, son of Poseidon and the nymph Thoosa, is the one-eyed giant Odysseus later blinds. In a story told by Theocritus and Ovid, he falls hopelessly in love with the sea nymph Galatea. Redon shows the giant peering shyly over a hill at the sleeping nymph, turning a monster into a lonely, almost gentle figure.
