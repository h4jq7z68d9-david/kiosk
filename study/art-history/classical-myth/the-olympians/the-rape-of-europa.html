Titian

The Rape of Europa

1560–62

Oil on canvas · Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum, Boston

Made for: The last of the poesie painted for Philip II of Spain.

Zeus turned himself into a beautiful white bull, coaxed the Phoenician princess Europa onto his back, and swam with her across the sea to Crete. There she bore Minos, the future king of Crete, whose palace and Labyrinth return in the story of Theseus. Titian shows her sprawled and terrified as the shore recedes, with cupids riding the waves.
