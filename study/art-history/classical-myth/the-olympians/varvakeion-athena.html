Varvakeion Athena

Roman copy, ca. 200–250 CE, of the Athena Parthenos (438 BCE)

Marble · National Archaeological Museum, Athens

Made for: A small Roman copy of the lost cult statue that stood inside the Parthenon.

Phidias’s Athena Parthenos was about 40 feet tall, built of gold and ivory over a wooden core, and none of it survives. This small copy, found near the Varvakeion school in Athens, preserves its main features: a triple-crested helmet, the aegis with the Gorgon’s head, a figure of Victory in her right hand, and a shield and serpent at her side.

  • Copy of Phidias’s lost cult statue
  • Helmet, aegis, Victory, shield, serpent
  • Original: gold and ivory, ~40 ft
