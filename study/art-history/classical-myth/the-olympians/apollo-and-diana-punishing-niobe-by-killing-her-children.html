Abraham Bloemaert

Apollo and Diana Punishing Niobe by Killing Her Children

1591

Oil on canvas · Statens Museum for Kunst, Copenhagen

Made for: An early showpiece by the Utrecht painter, made to display his skill at twisting nude figures in the elegant Mannerist style.

Queen Niobe of Thebes boasted that her fourteen children made her greater than Leto, who had only two. Leto’s twins, Apollo and Artemis, shot down all of Niobe’s children with arrows. Bloemaert puts the archer gods in the clouds and fills the ground with the bodies of the young; Niobe’s grief turned her to stone on Mount Sipylus, still weeping as a spring.

  • Niobe boasts over Leto
  • Apollo and Artemis kill her children
  • Turned to weeping stone
