The Olympians · Hermes, Hephaestus, Ares, and the Goddesses

Diego Velázquez

The Spinners (The Fable of Arachne)

ca. 1655–60

Oil on canvas · Museo del Prado, Madrid

Made for: Painted for a private collector in Madrid; it later entered the royal collection.

The Lydian weaver Arachne boasted that she wove better than Athena. In their contest, she wove the gods’ scandalous love affairs so perfectly that Athena struck her, and Arachne was turned into a spider. Velázquez puts working women spinning in the foreground and the myth in the bright room behind, where the tapestry copies Titian’s Rape of Europa.

  • Arachne challenges Athena
  • Turned into a spider
  • Myth hidden behind a workshop scene
Arachne
