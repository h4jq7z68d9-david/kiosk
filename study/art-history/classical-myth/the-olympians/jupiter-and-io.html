The Olympians · Zeus, Hera, Poseidon, and Apollo

Correggio

Jupiter and Io

ca. 1531–32

Oil on canvas · Kunsthistorisches Museum, Vienna

Made for: Companion to the Ganymede in the same Loves of Jupiter series for the Gonzaga court.

To hide his affair with the priestess Io from Hera, Zeus wrapped himself in a dark cloud; Correggio shows only a faint face and a hand emerging from the mist as Io melts into his embrace. When Hera grew suspicious, Zeus turned Io into a white cow, and Hera set the hundred-eyed giant Argus to guard her.

  • Zeus disguised as a cloud
  • Io turned into a cow
  • Hera sets Argus to guard her
Io
Priestess of Hera loved by Zeus and turned into a cow; she wandered as far as Egypt before regaining her form.
