Jacques-Louis David

Andromache Mourning Hector

1783

Oil on canvas · École des Beaux-Arts, Paris, on deposit at the Louvre

Made for: The painting David submitted to win admission to the French Royal Academy, shown at the Salon of 1783.

Hector, Troy’s greatest defender, killed Patroclus. Grief-stricken, Achilles returned to battle in new armor made by Hephaestus, killed Hector, and dragged his body behind his chariot. Hector’s wife Andromache and their little son Astyanax lost everything. David shows Andromache beside Hector’s body, one hand reaching toward him and the other holding her child.
