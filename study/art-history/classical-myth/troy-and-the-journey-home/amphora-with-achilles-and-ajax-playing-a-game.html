Amphora with Achilles and Ajax playing a game

Athenian, ca. 540–530 BCE

Black-figure terracotta · Vatican Museums

Made for: A storage jar signed by Exekias as both potter and painter, found in an Etruscan tomb at Vulci.

Achilles, raised by the wise centaur Chiron, was the greatest Greek warrior; his cousin Ajax was the next. Exekias imagines them in a quiet moment, leaning over a board game during a lull in the fighting, spears on their shoulders. Inscriptions give their throws, and Achilles, still wearing his helmet, is winning. The calm makes the violence everyone knows is coming more powerful.
