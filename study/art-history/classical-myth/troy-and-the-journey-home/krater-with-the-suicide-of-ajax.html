Krater with the Suicide of Ajax

Etruscan, ca. 400–350 BCE

Red-figure calyx krater, said to be from Vulci · British Museum, London

Made for: A wine-mixing bowl made in Etruria, showing how Greek myths spread to Italy.

After Achilles was killed by an arrow from Paris, guided by Apollo, the Greeks awarded his armor to Odysseus instead of Ajax. Humiliated, Ajax went mad, then killed himself by falling on his sword. Earlier, Achilles had killed the Amazon queen Penthesilea and fallen in love with her as she died, and mocked by the soldier Thersites, killed him too. The painting shows Ajax kneeling over the sword fixed in the ground.

  • Death of Achilles
  • Armor given to Odysseus
  • Ajax falls on his sword
Penthesilea
Amazon queen and ally of Troy, killed by Achilles.
Thersites
Common soldier who mocked the leaders and was struck down by Achilles.
Armor of Achilles
The prize whose award to Odysseus drove Ajax to suicide.
