Troy and the Odyssey

The war over Helen, and Odysseus’s long voyage home

The Trojan War began with a wedding, a golden apple and a beauty contest, and ended ten years later with a wooden horse. Homer’s Iliad tells one episode of the war; his Odyssey follows the hero Odysseus on his ten-year journey home.

  • Greek vase painters told the Trojan stories in close, labelled detail.
  • Later painters chose the moments of grief and fate.
  • Odysseus’s wit, not strength, carries him through monsters and enchantresses.
