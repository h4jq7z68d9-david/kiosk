Heroes of Argos · Perseus and the Heroes of Argos

Edward Burne-Jones

The Baleful Head

1885

Gouache · Southampton City Art Gallery

Made for: One of the gouache designs for Burne-Jones’s Perseus cycle, commissioned by Arthur Balfour for the music room of his London house; this one plans the cycle’s last scene.

The cycle ends with Perseus showing Andromeda Medusa’s head reflected in the still water of a well, so she can see it safely. Burne-Jones turns the story’s violence into a hushed, dreamy love scene among apple trees. The mirror motif ties the ending back to the shield Perseus used to approach Medusa.

  • Medusa’s head seen in a well’s reflection
  • Violence turned into quiet romance
