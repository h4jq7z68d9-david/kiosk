Gian Lorenzo Bernini (with Pietro Bernini)

Aeneas, Anchises, and Ascanius

1618–19

Marble · Galleria Borghese, Rome

Made for: Commissioned by Cardinal Scipione Borghese for his villa; it was the young Bernini’s first life-size marble group.

Aeneas, son of Venus and the Trojan Anchises, escapes burning Troy carrying his old father, who holds the household gods, and leading his small son Ascanius, also called Iulus. Virgil wrote the Aeneid under Augustus, whose family, the Julii, claimed descent from Iulus. Bernini stacks the three generations into one spiral: the old man, the hero in his prime, and the child. The group is the image of Roman pietas.

  • Aeneas carries Anchises, leads Ascanius
  • Iulus: ancestor of the Julian family
  • Three generations = pietas
