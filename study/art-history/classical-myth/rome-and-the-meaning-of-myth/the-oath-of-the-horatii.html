Jacques-Louis David

The Oath of the Horatii

1784–85

Oil on canvas · Louvre, Paris

Made for: Commissioned by the French crown; its message of patriotic sacrifice was later read as a call to revolution.

In the war between Rome and Alba Longa, the two cities let three brothers from each side, the Roman Horatii and the Alban Curiatii, fight for them. David shows the Horatii swearing on swords held by their father, while the women, one engaged to a Curiatius, collapse in grief. Only one Horatius survived; when his sister mourned her dead fiancé, he killed her too.
