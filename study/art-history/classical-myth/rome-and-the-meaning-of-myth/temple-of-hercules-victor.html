Temple of Hercules Victor

Late 2nd century BCE

Marble · Forum Boarium, Rome

Made for: A round temple in the cattle market beside the Tiber, near the Great Altar where Romans honored Hercules.

Romans told that Hercules, driving Geryon’s cattle back from Spain, stopped at the site of Rome, where the monster Cacus stole some of the herd. Hercules killed him, and King Evander set up the Ara Maxima, the Great Altar, in his honor. The cattle market, the Forum Boarium, grew up around it. This marble temple is one of the oldest surviving in Rome.

  • Hercules kills Cacus at Rome
  • Ara Maxima founded by Evander
