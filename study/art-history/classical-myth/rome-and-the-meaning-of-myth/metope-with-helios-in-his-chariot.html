Rome and the Meaning of Myth · Interpreting Myth

Metope with Helios in his chariot

Hellenistic, ca. 300–280 BCE

Marble, from the Temple of Athena at Ilion (Troy) · Altes Museum, Berlin

Made for: A carved panel from the temple of Athena at Hellenistic Troy, found by Heinrich Schliemann.

The sun god rises in his four-horse chariot, his head crowned with rays. In the 1800s the philologist Max Müller argued that most myths began as poetic descriptions of the sun, dawn, and sky, misunderstood as language changed, so that a story like Daphne fleeing Apollo meant the dawn fleeing the sun. Solar mythology faded, but comparing myths across Indo-European languages remained influential, notably in Georges Dumézil’s theory of three social functions.
