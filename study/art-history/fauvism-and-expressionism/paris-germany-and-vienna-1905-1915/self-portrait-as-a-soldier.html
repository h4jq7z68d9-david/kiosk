Paris, Germany and Vienna, 1905–1915

Ernst Ludwig Kirchner

Self-Portrait as a Soldier

1915

Oil on canvas, 27 1/4 × 25 in. Allen Memorial Art Museum, Oberlin College, Oberlin, Ohio

The face and uniform are cut into sharp planes, and the paint is dragged in hatched strokes that leave every edge hard.

Look at

  • The face is a yellow-ochre wedge with sunken blue eyes and a hard shadow down the nose.
  • The right sleeve ends in a flat red stump with no hand.
  • Blue uniform, orange wall and yellow skin are set side by side at full strength, with no blending.
  • The nude behind is painted in the same ochre as the face, so the two figures read as one colour against the dark ground.
  • Kirchner was inducted in 1915 and released for illness; in 1937 the painting hung in the Munich exhibition the Nazis called Degenerate Art.
