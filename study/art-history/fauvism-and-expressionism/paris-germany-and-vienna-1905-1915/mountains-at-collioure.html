André Derain

Mountains at Collioure

1905

Oil on canvas, 32 × 39 1/2 in. National Gallery of Art, Washington

André Derain (French, 1880–1954). The trees are made of separate dashes of pure blue, green and yellow, laid side by side without touching.

Look at

  • Each dash is one flat colour, so the foliage reads as a mosaic.
  • The tree trunks are bright red-orange lines, and the hills behind are flat zones of orange, blue and violet.
  • The sky is pale green and yellow with white clouds drawn in long curving strokes.
  • Nothing is shaded: light and shadow are replaced by changes of colour.
  • Derain and Matisse worked side by side at Collioure in summer 1905.
