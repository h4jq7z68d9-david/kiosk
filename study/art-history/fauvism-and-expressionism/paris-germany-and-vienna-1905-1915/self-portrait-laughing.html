Richard Gerstl

Self-Portrait, Laughing

1908

Oil on canvas, 15 3/4 × 12 in. Belvedere, Vienna

Richard Gerstl (Austrian, 1883–1908). The paint is smeared and dabbed so thickly that the face is half dissolved into the brown dots behind it.

Look at

  • The background is a field of brown, ochre and green dabs, each a separate thick touch.
  • The face is smeared rather than dabbed, and its edges blur into the background at the right cheek and chin.
  • The open mouth and the two eyes are the only sharply drawn passages.
  • The jacket is a few dark smears at the bottom, barely indicated.
  • Gerstl taught himself to paint, kept apart from Vienna's artist groups and died at twenty-five; the museum reads this laugh as more menacing than cheerful.
