Henri Matisse

Open Window, Collioure

1905

Oil on canvas, 21 3/4 × 18 in. National Gallery of Art, Washington

The paint is laid on in loose, separate strokes, and bare canvas shows between them across much of the picture.

Look at

  • The view through the window is a patchwork of short pink, blue and white dabs for sea, sky and boats.
  • The left wall is green and the right wall is pink, two sides of the same room in opposite colours.
  • The window frames are striped in red, green and blue rather than painted as wood.
  • The flowerpots and leaves at the bottom are blobs of red, green and blue, each one or two strokes.
  • Shown at the 1905 Salon d'Automne; the view is of sailboats off Collioure, a town on the Mediterranean coast.
