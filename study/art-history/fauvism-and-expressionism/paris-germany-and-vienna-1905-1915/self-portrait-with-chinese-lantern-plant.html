Egon Schiele

Self-Portrait with Chinese Lantern Plant

1912

Oil and opaque colour on wood, 12 3/4 × 15 3/4 in. Leopold Museum, Vienna

Egon Schiele (Austrian, 1890–1918). A hard, wiry contour runs around the head and shoulders, set against an almost empty pale ground.

Look at

  • The face is drawn with a fine dark line and shaded in streaks of grey, green and red, so the skin looks bruised rather than lit.
  • The head tilts one way and the eyes look the other, straight at the viewer.
  • The background is flat off-white with the brushstrokes showing, and the red lantern fruits on the left are the only bright colour.
  • The top edge cuts through the hair and the bottom edge through the chest, so the figure is cropped close.
  • Painted as a pair with Schiele's portrait of Wally Neuzil, which the museum says accounts for the odd framing of both pictures.
