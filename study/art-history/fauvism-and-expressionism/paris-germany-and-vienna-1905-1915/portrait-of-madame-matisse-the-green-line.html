Henri Matisse

Portrait of Madame Matisse. The Green Line

1905

Oil on canvas, 16 × 12 3/4 in. Statens Museum for Kunst, Copenhagen

A single stripe of green runs down the middle of the face and splits it into a warm yellow half and a cool pink half.

Look at

  • The green line runs from the hairline down the nose to the chin, and the yellow and pink halves meet along it without blending.
  • The background is cut into three flat fields: violet, orange-red and green, each meeting the next with a hard edge.
  • The hair is one dark blue mass with a few lighter strokes, not drawn strand by strand.
  • Thick strokes cover the whole surface, the background laid on as heavily as the face.
  • The museum dates it, with a 'presumably', to autumn 1905, after the summer at Collioure.
