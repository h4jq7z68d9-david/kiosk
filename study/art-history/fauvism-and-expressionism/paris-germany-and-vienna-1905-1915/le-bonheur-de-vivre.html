Henri Matisse

Le Bonheur de vivre

1905–06

Oil on canvas, 69 1/2 × 94 3/4 in. Barnes Foundation, Philadelphia

The figures and trees are flat areas of colour held inside long curving outlines, with almost no modelling.

Look at

  • The reclining women are pale pink shapes edged in red or green.
  • Tree trunks bend in sweeping arcs, and the foliage above is a set of flat green, pink and orange patches.
  • The ground shifts from yellow to orange to green in broad zones, each colour laid flat.
  • A ring of small dancers in the distance is painted as a loop of outlines on the yellow field.
  • Painted between October 1905 and March 1906 and shown at the 1906 Salon des Indépendants; bought there by Leo Stein.
