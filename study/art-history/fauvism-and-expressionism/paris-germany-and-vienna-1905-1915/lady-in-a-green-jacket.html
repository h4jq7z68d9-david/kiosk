August Macke

Lady in a Green Jacket

1913

Oil on canvas, 17 1/4 × 17 in. Museum Ludwig, Cologne

August Macke (German, 1887–1914). The figures are smooth, rounded colour shapes with no faces, set in a landscape of equally smooth blocks.

Look at

  • The woman in green is seen from behind, her jacket one curved green form over a dark skirt.
  • The couple at the left and the woman in violet at the right are painted the same way, as coloured silhouettes.
  • The lake is a flat blue band, the path and houses are yellow and ochre blocks, and the trees are circles of green.
  • The surface is brushed in small, even strokes, so even the brightest colours stay calm.
  • Bought in 1947 for the Wallraf-Richartz-Museum in Cologne and transferred to the Museum Ludwig in 1976.
