Dresden and Berlin

Max Pechstein

Elias Cemetery in Dresden

1906

Oil on canvas, 44.3 × 61 cm. Brücke-Museum, Berlin

An old Dresden graveyard in deep green, with headstones, a cross and trees, and no people anywhere in view. A quiet, still subject is given a restless, heavy surface.

  • He signed it at lower right with a small monogram and the year.
  • Look at the lawn: it rolls in wavy bands, as if the ground itself were moving.
  • Thick paint goes on in short, curving strokes that follow the shapes of leaves and stones.
  • The museum's record tags Van Gogh as a reference. That borrowed energy unsettles a resting place, so mood counts for more than an exact view.
  • The museum acquired it from a private collection in 1979.
