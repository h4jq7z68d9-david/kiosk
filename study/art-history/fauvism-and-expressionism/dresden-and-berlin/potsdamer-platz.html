Dresden and Berlin

Ernst Ludwig Kirchner

Potsdamer Platz

1914

Oil on canvas, 200 × 150 cm. Nationalgalerie, Staatliche Museen zu Berlin, Berlin

Two women stand on a round traffic island while men in dark suits pass along the street behind them. It is the largest of the painter's city scenes.

  • Real landmarks appear, among them the domed Café Piccadilly at left and the entrance to the Potsdam railway station.
  • Look at the green street rising steeply behind the women, as if seen from above.
  • The black widow's veil on the left-hand woman was painted in after the war began.
  • The two women seem cut off from the passers-by around them. Warped space and unnatural color make a busy square feel isolating.
  • The museum bought it in 1999 with help from several German cultural foundations and the federal government.
