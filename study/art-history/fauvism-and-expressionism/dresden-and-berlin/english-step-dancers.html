Ernst Ludwig Kirchner

English Step Dancers

1911

Color woodcut printed in black, yellow, green and pink on blotting paper, image 40.5 × 27.5 cm. National Gallery of Art, Washington

Two dancers in matching dark costumes step forward together, one hand on the other's shoulder, beside a large potted plant. It carries the stage theme from canvas into print.

  • Inscribed "Eigendruck," meaning the artist printed this impression himself.
  • Look at the edges: the green and yellow areas do not quite meet the black outlines.
  • The soft, absorbent paper soaks up the ink, giving the colors a matte, uneven surface.
  • The bodies are cut into flat blocks of color, so the handmade process stays in plain view rather than being polished away.
  • Given to the Jena art society in 1918, it was taken in the 1937 purge of "degenerate art" and then sold abroad.
Woodcut
A print made by cutting away a wood block so that only the raised parts take ink.
