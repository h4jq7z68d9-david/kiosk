Käthe Kollwitz

Outbreak (Losbruch), plate 5 of Peasants' War

1902–03

Line etching, drypoint, aquatint, emery and soft ground, corrected with pencil and opaque color, sheet 50.2 × 58.7 cm. Kupferstichkabinett, Staatliche Museen zu Berlin, Berlin

A woman flings up her arms as armed peasants surge past her into revolt. This sheet is a working proof of the second state, not a finished edition print.

  • She came to the subject in 1901 through Wilhelm Zimmermann's account of the Peasants' War.
  • Though numbered fifth, it was the first plate of the cycle she finished.
  • Look at the woman's back, turned to us, so her raised arms seem to launch the crowd forward.
  • Rather than illustrating a text, this history print centers on rising up against injustice, giving an old revolt a social purpose.
  • The leading woman is "Black Anna," a figure from the revolt; this is probably the cycle's only plate showing a known person.
Etching
A print made from a metal plate whose lines are bitten into the metal with acid.
Aquatint
An etching method that bites areas of tone, not lines, into the plate.
