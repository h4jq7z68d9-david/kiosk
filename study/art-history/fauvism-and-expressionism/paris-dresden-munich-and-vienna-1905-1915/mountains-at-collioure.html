André Derain

Mountains at Collioure

1905

French, 1880–1954. Oil on canvas, 32 × 39 1/2 in. National Gallery of Art, Washington

32 × 39 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Derain made the trees from separate dashes of pure blue, green, and yellow, with pale ground showing between them. Even the darkest dashes are deep blue and blue-green, not black or brown, so the shaded foliage stays strongly colored.

  • Derain and Matisse worked side by side at Collioure in summer 1905.
