Adélaïde Labille-Guiard

Self-Portrait with Two Pupils

1785

French, 1749–1803. Oil on canvas, 83 × 59 1/2 in. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

83 × 59 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Labille-Guiard painted satin, lace, straw, and skin each at full sharpness. Crisp, near-white highlights make the silver-blue silk read as stiff, shiny satin.

  • A painter at her easel with her two students, shown at the Salon of 1785.
