Raphael

The School of Athens

about 1509–11

Italian, 1483–1520. Fresco, about 196 3/4 × 303 1/4 in. Stanza della Segnatura, Vatican

about 196 3/4 × 303 1/4 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

The Vatican Museums date the start of the High Renaissance to the frescoes in this room. The arches shrink, one inside the next, toward two men at the center.

  • Painted for Pope Julius II in a room he used as his library. The painted hall was inspired by Bramante's design for rebuilding St. Peter's. Raphael's cartoon for the figures survives in Milan.
