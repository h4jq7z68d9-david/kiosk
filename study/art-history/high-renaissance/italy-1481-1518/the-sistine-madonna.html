Raphael

The Sistine Madonna

1512–13

Oil on canvas, 106 × 79 1/4 in. Gemäldegalerie Alte Meister, Dresden

106 × 79 1/4 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Curtains drawn back at the top and a ledge at the bottom make the scene look like a vision. Since about 1800, the two small angels on the ledge have been copied on their own.

  • Raphael made it for the high altar of the convent church of San Sisto in Piacenza, most likely on the order of Pope Julius II. Raphael added the two angels last.
