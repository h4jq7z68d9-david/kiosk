Michelangelo

The Libyan Sibyl

about 1511–12

Fresco, about 178 3/4 × 149 1/2 in. (her section of the ceiling). Sistine Chapel ceiling, Vatican

about 178 3/4 × 149 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

The shoulders turn away while the knees face front, so the seated figure looks caught in the middle of a turn. Michelangelo's color in the second half of the ceiling influenced many of the painters later called Mannerists.

  • Michelangelo painted her in about twenty daily sections of plaster. A red chalk study for the pose, drawn from a male model, is in the Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York.
