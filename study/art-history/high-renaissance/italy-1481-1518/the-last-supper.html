Leonardo da Vinci

The Last Supper

about 1494–98

Italian, 1452–1519. Tempera and oil on plaster, about 181 × 346 1/2 in. Refectory of Santa Maria delle Grazie, Milan

about 181 × 346 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

This painting began a new era in art. The receding lines of the painted room run toward Christ's head, so the architecture itself points at him.

  • Leonardo painted on dry plaster instead of wet, which let him work slowly and change what he had done. The paint began to flake within a few years; a restoration finished in 1999 removed later repainting.
