Leonardo da Vinci

Mona Lisa

1503–19

Oil on poplar panel, 31 1/4 × 21 in. Musée du Louvre, Paris

31 1/4 × 21 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

The edges of the face fade into shadow with no drawn line, a method called sfumato. Painters of the time, the young Raphael among them, drew on this portrait for their own.

  • Leonardo began it by October 1503 and had not finished it when he died. Old, yellowed varnish now turns the blue of the sky toward green and darkens the lower part.
