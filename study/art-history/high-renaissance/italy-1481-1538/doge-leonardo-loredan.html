Giovanni Bellini

Doge Leonardo Loredan

about 1501–2

Italian, about 1435–1516. Oil on poplar panel, 24 1/4 × 17 1/2 in. The National Gallery, London

Bellini blended slow-drying oil paint in the face, so wrinkles and loose skin turn gradually from light into shadow.

  • Light falls from the left and the right side of the face sinks into shade, so the head looks round against the flat blue.
  • Short, thick strokes pick out the gold thread on the white silk, so the robe looks stiff and shiny.
  • Loredan was elected doge, the head of the Venetian state, in 1501. The blue behind him is ultramarine, a costly pigment, mixed with white.
