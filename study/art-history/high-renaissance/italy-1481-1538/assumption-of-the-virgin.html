Titian

Assumption of the Virgin

1516–18

Italian, about 1488/90–1576. Oil on panel, 263 × 135 1/2 in. Santa Maria Gloriosa dei Frari, Venice

Raised arms link its three tiers of figures, so the eye climbs from the apostles on the ground to the top.

  • Unveiled on the high altar of the Frari, a Franciscan church, on May 19, 1518. The figure of God at the top, far above the viewer, is painted boldly with thick, unblended highlights.
