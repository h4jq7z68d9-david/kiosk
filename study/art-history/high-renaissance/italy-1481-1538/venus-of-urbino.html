Italy, 1481–1538

Titian

Venus of Urbino

1538

Oil on canvas, 46 3/4 × 65 in. The Uffizi, Florence

Titian set warm, softly shaded skin against white sheets and a dark screen, so the body stands out from the room.

  • The dark screen ends just past the hips, so the pale head and torso stand out sharply against it.
  • Red repeats in the mattress, the roses and the maid's dress, so the near bed and the far room tie together.
  • Guidobaldo II della Rovere bought the picture directly from Titian. It hung for many years in the Uffizi near an ancient marble Venus, probably so the two could be compared.
