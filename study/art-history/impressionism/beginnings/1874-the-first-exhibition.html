1874: The First Exhibition

An artist-run show, and a mocking new name

In the spring of 1874 Monet, Renoir, Degas, Morisot, Pissarro, Sisley and Cézanne held their own show, alongside two dozen other artists, most of them little known today.

  • The show ran from 15 April to 15 May 1874 on the boulevard des Capucines.
  • The artists ran it through a société anonyme, a company they formed, and showed about 200 works.
  • Little sold, but the press covered the show widely.
