Modern Paris: Boulevards, Leisure and Labor

Where Parisians worked and played

In the mid-1800s Paris was rebuilt with broad boulevards, railway stations, cafés and theaters. The Impressionists and painters close to them made this new city their subject. These ten pictures, from about 1874 to 1897, show its crowds, pleasures and workers.

  • Baron Haussmann's rebuilding replaced old streets with wide avenues and uniform apartment blocks.
  • The third Impressionist exhibition, in April 1877, showed several of these works together.
