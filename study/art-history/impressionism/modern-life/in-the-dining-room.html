Modern Life

Berthe Morisot

In the Dining Room

1886

Oil on canvas, 61.3 × 50 cm. National Gallery of Art, Washington

A young maid in a dark dress and white apron stands in a dining room and looks straight out. Morisot paints the room around her in loose, rapid strokes.

  • A maid, not the family she serves, fills the picture in an ordinary working moment.
  • Shown at the last Impressionist exhibition in 1886, listed as Petite servante (Little Servant).
  • A cabinet of white and silver pieces stands at left, a round table at right.
  • Look at the lower edge, where a small spotted dog runs past her feet.
  • Chester Dale's bequest brought it to the National Gallery of Art in 1963.

Paris households of Morisot's class kept live-in servants. They cooked, cleaned and waited at table.
