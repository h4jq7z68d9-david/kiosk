Pierre-Auguste Renoir

Bal du moulin de la Galette

1876

French, 1841–1919. Oil on canvas, 51 3/4 × 69 1/2 in. Musée d'Orsay, Paris

51 3/4 × 69 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Renoir painted sunlight through leaves as loose, pale patches laid across the dancers. Strokes of blue, pink, and yellow repeat across the whole crowd, tying it together.

  • Shown at the third Impressionist exhibition, 1877, where critics objected to the blurred forms. Some of Renoir's friends posed for it.
