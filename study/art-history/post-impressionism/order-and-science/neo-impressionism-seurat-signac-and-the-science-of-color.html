Neo-Impressionism: Seurat, Signac and the Science of Color

Theory turned into a working method.

From the mid-1880s a group of painters set pure color down in separate touches. Within a few years the approach had followers across France and in Belgium.

  • Seurat's large figure paintings, from the riverbank to the circus ring
  • Signac, the movement's champion
  • Followers: Cross, Luce and Van Rysselberghe
Neo-Impressionism
The critic Félix Fénéon's 1886 name for the color method of Seurat, Signac and their circle.
