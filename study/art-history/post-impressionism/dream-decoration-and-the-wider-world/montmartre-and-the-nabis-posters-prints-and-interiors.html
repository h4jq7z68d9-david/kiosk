Montmartre and the Nabis: Posters, Prints and Interiors

From café-concert billboards to quiet rooms covered in pattern

Montmartre's dance halls and cabarets hired painters to design posters for their stars. At the same time, a group of young friends made small, private pictures of family rooms.

  • Posters and paintings of Montmartre nightlife, its performers and its audiences
  • The Nabis' decorative panels and self-portraits
  • Color prints published by Ambroise Vollard, and a group portrait
