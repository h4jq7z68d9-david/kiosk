Symbolism in France: Puvis, Redon and Rousseau

Painters who turned from the visible world to dreams, ideas and imagined places.

In the 1880s and 1890s some French artists left the viewer to complete the meaning.

  • A calm, simplified figure painting.
  • Prints and drawings in black that picture visions, evolution and literature, then a late turn to color.
  • Jungle and desert scenes by a self-taught painter, built from sources found in Paris.
Symbolism
A movement in art and poetry that sought to evoke ideas and inner states instead of describing the visible world.
