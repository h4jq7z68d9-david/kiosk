Georges Seurat

Bathers at Asnières

1884

French, 1859–1891. Oil on canvas, 79 1/4 × 118 in. The National Gallery, London

79 1/4 × 118 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Seurat built the surface from visible dabs, flicks, and crisscross strokes and held every figure as still as a statue. Most of the large figures sit in profile inside unbroken outlines, so each reads as a fixed shape.

  • Rejected by the official Salon and shown instead at the first Salon des Indépendants, a jury-free exhibition, in 1884. Seurat later reworked the orange hat in dots of yellow and blue.
