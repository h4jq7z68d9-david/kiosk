Paul Cézanne

The Basket of Apples

about 1893

French, 1839–1906. Oil on canvas, 25 1/2 × 31 1/2 in. The Art Institute of Chicago

25 1/2 × 31 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Cézanne painted the table as if from two places at once: its front edge sits lower on the left of the cloth than on the right. The basket tips forward and the bottle leans left, so each object looks as if seen from a slightly different spot.

  • Shown at Cézanne's first one-man exhibition, at the Vollard gallery in Paris, 1895.
