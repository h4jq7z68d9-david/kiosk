David and the Revolution

From the royal commission to the consulate, 1782–1806

In these works the classical style moves from large public history pictures into portraits and more private images.

  • Greek and Roman stories on large canvases
  • Self-portraits by three painters
  • Two works left unfinished
  • Two women painters of the 1780s
  • A marble that reworks the antique, and a moonlit nude
