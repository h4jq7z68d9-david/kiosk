Hans Holbein the Younger

The Ambassadors

1533

German, 1497/8–1543. Oil on oak, 81 1/2 × 82 1/2 in. National Gallery, London

81 1/2 × 82 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

The long smear across the floor is a skull, stretched so far that the shape reads only from a sharp angle.

  • The trick is called anamorphosis. The sitters are Jean de Dinteville, French ambassador to England, and his friend Georges de Selve, a bishop; the skull is a reminder of death.
