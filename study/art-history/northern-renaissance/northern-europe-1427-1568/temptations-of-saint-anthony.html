Hieronymus Bosch

Temptations of Saint Anthony

about 1500

Oil on oak; center panel 51 3/4 × 46 3/4 in., wings 51 3/4 × 20 3/4 in. Museu Nacional de Arte Antiga, Lisbon

Sky, land, water and fire are packed into one dark landscape and filled with creatures built from mismatched parts.

  • Orange flames from a burning village light the upper left of the center panel, so that corner pulls the eye in a dark picture.
  • Small patches of bright red recur in all three panels, so the eye jumps from one group of creatures to the next.
  • Signed at the lower left of the center panel. Closed, the wings show Christ on the way from his arrest to Calvary, painted almost without color.
