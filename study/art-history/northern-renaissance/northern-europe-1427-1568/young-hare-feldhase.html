Northern Europe, 1427–1568

Albrecht Dürer

Young Hare (Feldhase)

1502

Watercolor and bodycolor with white highlights, 10 × 9 in. Albertina, Vienna

Watercolor and opaque paint together make fur so fine that each hair seems to be there.

  • Light comes from the left and the shadow falls to the right, so the body looks round and seems to rest on a surface.
  • The paper around the animal is left blank, so nothing competes with the fur.
  • Bodycolor is opaque water-based paint. The eye reflects the bars of a window, and so many copies were made that at least 25 hares were once in European collections.
