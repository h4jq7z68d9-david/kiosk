Hugo van der Goes

Portinari Triptych

about 1477–78

Flemish, about 1440–1482. Oil on wood, 107 3/4 × 256 3/4 in. open. Uffizi, Florence

Figures in one scene are painted at different sizes: saints tower over the kneeling family they protect.

  • The standing saints on the wings are far taller than the donors kneeling below them, so rank shows at a glance.
  • A wide patch of bare ground is left around the child, so the small pale body is the first thing seen in the center.
  • Tommaso Portinari, agent of the Medici bank in Bruges, ordered it for a hospital church in Florence. It arrived by sea in 1483, and Florentine painters admired its realistic objects, landscape and faces.
