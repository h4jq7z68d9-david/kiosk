Isenheim Altarpiece: The Crucifixion

1512–16

Born in Würzburg about 1475–80, died 1528. Oil and tempera on limewood. Musée Unterlinden, Colmar

Christ's body is gray-green, torn and stuck with thorns, and hangs against a dark night sky.

  • The arms pull the crossbar down into a bow and the fingers splay stiffly, so the body looks heavy.
  • Red and white robes at the sides are the only bright color, so the pale body stands alone at the center.
  • Painted for the hospital chapel of the Antonite monks at Isenheim, who nursed people with a disease that caused gangrene. This view showed when the altarpiece was closed.
