Hieronymus Bosch

The Haywain Triptych

1512–15

Oil on panel, 58 × 88 1/4 in. with frame. Museo del Prado, Madrid

Crowds of small figures are set down fast, some faces just a few dots, across three panels that end in Hell.

  • Green hills fill the left and center panels and black and red fill the right, so the last panel breaks away from the others.
  • The pale yellow haystack fills the middle of the center panel, so the crowd reads as one mass pulled toward the hay.
  • The cart comes from a Bible verse and a Flemish saying that everyone grabs as much as they can. A second signed version at El Escorial is a copy of this one.
