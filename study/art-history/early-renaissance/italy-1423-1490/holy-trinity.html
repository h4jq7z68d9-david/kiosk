Masaccio

Holy Trinity

about 1425–27

Fresco. Santa Maria Novella, Florence

The vault's lines meet at one low point, a method called linear perspective, and the flat wall looks like a deep chapel.

  • The sunken squares in the ceiling shrink row by row toward the back, so the vault looks like it runs deep behind the figures.
  • The two kneeling figures overlap the painted columns, so they look closer to the viewer than the chapel behind them.
  • Counted among the first major paintings built on perspective. In 1570 it was hidden behind a stone altar; since 1952 it has been back in its first place on the church wall.
