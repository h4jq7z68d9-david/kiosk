Italy, 1423–1490

Giovanni Bellini

St. Francis in the Desert

about 1475–80

Italian, about 1424/35–1516. Oil on panel, 49 × 56 in. The Frick Collection, New York

Bellini gave the rock, the town and the saint the same light, so the whole landscape looks like one place at one hour.

  • The cliff is blue-gray in shade and cream where the sun hits, so the rock looks lit from the upper left.
  • The saint stands low and right of center, well below the top of the cliff, so the landscape takes up most of the picture.
  • Bellini painted over a smooth white ground that reflects light back through the oil colors. The panel was later cut down, and who ordered the picture is not known.
