Domenico Ghirlandaio

Portrait of an Old Man and a Young Boy

1490

Italian, 1449–1494. Poplar wood panel, 24 3/4 × 18 1/4 in. Musée du Louvre, Paris

Ghirlandaio painted every bump and wrinkle of the old man's face and set it beside the smooth face of a child.

  • The growths on the nose and the mark on the forehead have their own small highlights, so the skin looks rough and real.
  • Both figures wear the same red, so the two heads read as one close group.
  • The sitters are not known, though names have been proposed.
