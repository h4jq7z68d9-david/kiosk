Masaccio

Holy Trinity

1425–26

Italian, 1401–1428 or 1429. Fresco, 262 1/2 × 124 3/4 in. Santa Maria Novella, Florence

262 1/2 × 124 3/4 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

The lines of the painted vault all run to one low point, near a standing viewer's eye level, so the flat wall seems to open into a deep chapel. The method is called linear perspective.

  • Counted among the first major paintings built on perspective. In 1570 it was hidden behind a stone altar; since 1952 it has been back on the stretch of church wall where it was first painted.
