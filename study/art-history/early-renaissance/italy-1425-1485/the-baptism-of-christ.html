Piero della Francesca

The Baptism of Christ

probably about 1437–45

Italian, about 1415/20–1492. Egg tempera on wood, 65 3/4 × 45 3/4 in. The National Gallery, London

65 3/4 × 45 3/4 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Piero set the dove, the water bowl, and Christ's joined hands one above another on the center line and painted everything in pale, even light, so the scene looks perfectly still.

  • Made for a church in Sansepolcro, Piero's hometown, and called his earliest surviving work. The hills and town in the background are read as Sansepolcro and its valley.
