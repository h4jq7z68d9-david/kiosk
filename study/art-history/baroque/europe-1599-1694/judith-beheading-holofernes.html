Artemisia Gentileschi

Judith Beheading Holofernes

about 1620

Italian, 1593–1652 or 1653. Oil on canvas, 78 1/4 × 64 in. The Uffizi, Florence

78 1/4 × 64 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

The three figures are cropped close and pushed right up to the front of the picture, and their arms lock together at the center, around the sword.

  • Made for Grand Duke Cosimo II de' Medici, it drew strong reactions in Florence and was denied a place in the Uffizi Gallery. Her friend Galileo helped her collect the late payment.
