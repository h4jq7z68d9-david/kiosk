Caravaggio

The Calling of Saint Matthew

1599–1600

Italian, 1571–1610. Oil on canvas, about 126 3/4 × 133 3/4 in. Contarelli Chapel, San Luigi dei Francesi, Rome

about 126 3/4 × 133 3/4 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

A single beam of light cuts across the dark room from the upper right and falls on the tax collectors at their table, while Christ, half hidden behind Peter, points toward Matthew. The men at the table wear the everyday clothes of Caravaggio's Rome.

  • Caravaggio signed the contract in July 1599. It was his first major public commission, one of three paintings of Saint Matthew for the same chapel, where it still hangs.
