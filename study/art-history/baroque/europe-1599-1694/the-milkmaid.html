Johannes Vermeer

The Milkmaid

about 1660

Dutch, 1632–1675. Oil on canvas, 18 × 16 1/4 in. Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam

18 × 16 1/4 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Daylight from the window at the left falls on a woman standing as still as a statue; only the stream of milk moves. Small dots of pale paint make the bread and basket glint.

  • Sold at auction in Amsterdam in 1696 for 175 guilders, it was bought for the Rijksmuseum in 1908 with help from the Rembrandt Association.
