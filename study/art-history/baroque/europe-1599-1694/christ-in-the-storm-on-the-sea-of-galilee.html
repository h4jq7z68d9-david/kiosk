Rembrandt

Christ in the Storm on the Sea of Galilee

1633

Dutch, 1606–1669. Oil on canvas, 63 × 50 1/2 in. Isabella Stewart Gardner Museum, Boston

63 × 50 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

A wave lifts the bow high on a steep tilt into a break of light in the clouds, while the back of the boat sinks into shadow.

  • Thieves stole it from the museum on March 18, 1990, cutting it from its frame; twelve other works were taken with it. It is still missing.
