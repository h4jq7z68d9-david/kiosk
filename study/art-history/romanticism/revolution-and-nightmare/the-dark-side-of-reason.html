The Dark Side of Reason

Wright of Derby, Fuseli, Blake, Martin, Palmer and Turner, 1768–1835

In the decades around 1800, British artists turned to dreams, visions and catastrophe, often against the confident science of their age. Some made pictures for London's crowded public exhibitions; others, like Blake, worked mainly for a few patrons and friends.

  • A forerunner: a science lesson lit by a single candle
  • Fuseli's bedroom demon and Blake's visionary prints and watercolors
  • A defiant poet, a moonlit harvest and a city fire as subjects for awe
