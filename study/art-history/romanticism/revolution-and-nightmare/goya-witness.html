Goya, Witness

Francisco de Goya, 1799–1823

Court painter to the Spanish crown, Goya lived through Napoleon's invasion of Spain and the brutal war that followed. His work moved from royal portraits to prints and private murals that face cruelty, fear and illness head on.

  • A state portrait at the court of Charles IV
  • Etched series on folly, war and its civilian cost
  • Late works made for himself and a friend
