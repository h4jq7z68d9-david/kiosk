Eugène Delacroix

Women of Algiers in Their Apartment

1834

Oil on canvas, 70 3/4 × 90 1/4 in. Musée du Louvre, Paris

Pattern covers nearly every surface: tiles, carpets, cushions and clothes.

Look at

  • Shadows are colored, not grey: blue on the tiled wall, deep red in the curtain.
  • Light enters from the left and falls on the reclining woman's cheek and blouse; half her face stays in shadow.
  • Small touches of orange, red and blue sit side by side in the fabrics.
  • The red cupboard doors are the strongest warm color, set left of center.
  • Three seated figures form a low row; a standing figure closes the right edge as one tall vertical.
  • Bought by the state at the Salon, the official French art exhibition, in the year the canvas was painted.
