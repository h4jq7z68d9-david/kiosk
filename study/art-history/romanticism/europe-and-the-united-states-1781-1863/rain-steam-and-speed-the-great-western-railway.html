Europe and the United States, 1781–1863

J.M.W. Turner

Rain, Steam and Speed – The Great Western Railway

1844

Oil on canvas, 35 3/4 × 48 in. The National Gallery, London

Almost everything is blurred into pale haze, with few hard edges.

Look at

  • The engine is the darkest and sharpest shape, a black funnel above a red-orange glow.
  • A dark bridge drives toward the viewer on a diagonal, its edges spreading apart toward the lower right corner.
  • Sky, rain and land share the same creams, golds and pale blues, with no clear horizon.
  • The sky is a field of loose, broken strokes.
  • A second bridge with arches sits small and pale at the far left.
  • A train crosses the Maidenhead railway bridge over the Thames; the bridge was finished in 1838.
