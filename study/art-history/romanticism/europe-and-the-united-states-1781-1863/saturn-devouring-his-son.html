Francisco de Goya

Saturn Devouring His Son

1820–23

Mixed technique, mural transferred to canvas, 56 1/2 × 32 in. Museo Nacional del Prado, Madrid

The body is distorted: limbs are angular and out of proportion, with no smooth outline.

Look at

  • The eyes are white circles with black dots, the sharpest marks in the picture.
  • The figure is roughly brushed in a few earth colors and seems to come out of solid black.
  • Red is kept to the small body and the fingers that grip it.
  • Broad strokes show in the hair, thigh and knee.
  • No floor, wall or horizon places the figure anywhere.
  • Painted straight onto a wall of the artist's house and later moved to canvas, with much paint lost in the transfer.
