Europe and the United States, 1781–1863

J.M.W. Turner

Slave Ship (Slavers Throwing Overboard the Dead and Dying, Typhoon Coming On)

1840

Oil on canvas, 35 3/4 × 48 1/4 in. Museum of Fine Arts, Boston

Paint is visibly dragged and layered across the whole surface, most of all in the white of the sky.

Look at

  • A vertical band of white and yellow light splits the picture just right of center.
  • The ship is a few thin dark lines for masts, half hidden by spray, left of center.
  • Color does the work of drawing: sea, sky and ship dissolve into strokes of red, orange, yellow and white.
  • The sea is brown, grey and gold, in heavy swirling strokes.
  • Small forms in the foreground water, a leg, hands, chains, fish and gulls, are the sharpest detail.
  • The title names the figures in the sea: people thrown overboard.
