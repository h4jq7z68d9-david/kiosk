Britain: Weather and Light

Turner, Constable, Barry and Pugin, 1812–c. 1870

In these decades British painters made sky, storm and changing daylight serious subjects for large exhibition pictures. Constable worked from close study of places he knew, while Turner pushed toward catastrophe, night and the machines of his own time.

  • Constable's Suffolk, Essex, Hampstead and Salisbury, sketched outdoors and worked up in the studio
  • Turner's Royal Academy pictures of war, the sea and the railway
  • A new Parliament built in a medieval style
