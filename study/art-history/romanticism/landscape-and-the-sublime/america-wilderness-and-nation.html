Landscape and the Sublime

America: Wilderness and Nation

Cole, Durand, Leutze, Lane, Church and Bierstadt, 1836–1863

In the United States, painters used landscape and history scenes to raise questions about the young country's land and its future. This part moves from Cole's moral allegories to the giant single-picture shows of Church and Bierstadt.

  • Wild land and cleared land as a question about the future
  • Painted cycles and history scenes that carry a lesson
  • Quiet harbor light and spectacular views of distant wonders
