France

Antoine-Jean Gros

Bonaparte Visiting the Plague-Stricken in Jaffa

1804

Oil on canvas, 523 × 715 cm. Musée du Louvre, Paris

A mosque courtyard serves as a military hospital for the Army of the Orient, and its commander walks among the dying. The picture links the grand style of David's school to the new regime of Napoleon.

  • Shown at the Salon of 1804. Bonaparte himself prompted the commission, which went through the interior ministry.
  • Look at his bare hand touching a sore on a patient's chest while an officer covers his nose.
  • Painted in a studio in the Jeu de Paume at Versailles and signed on a step at the left.
  • A living commander appears as a near-sacred healer, calm and upright among the dying, while a blind soldier at the right gropes his way toward him.
  • Paid for by the state in 1805, it sat in storage by 1821, reportedly deteriorating, then hung at the Luxembourg from 1830.
