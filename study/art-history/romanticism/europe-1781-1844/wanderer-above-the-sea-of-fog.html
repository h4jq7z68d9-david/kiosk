Caspar David Friedrich

Wanderer above the Sea of Fog

about 1817

German, 1774–1840. Oil on canvas, 37 1/4 × 29 1/2 in. Hamburger Kunsthalle, Hamburg

37 1/4 × 29 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Friedrich stood a single dark figure dead center, seen from behind. Distant ridges slope down from both sides and meet at his chest, so the eye slides along them to him.

  • A figure seen from behind, like this one, is called a Rückenfigur.
