Japanese Woodblock Prints

A compact guide to Japanese woodblock prints (ukiyo-e), c. 1680–1927, told through 64 works from open-access museum collections.

Japanese woodblock prints of the floating world, from the first black-line sheets of the 1680s through Hokusai, Hiroshige and the Meiji era, with Western works that drew on them and one 1927 print by Yoshida Hiroshi. Compiled from museum collection records, with lectures and reference essays as background.
