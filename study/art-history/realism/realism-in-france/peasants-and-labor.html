Realism in France

Peasants and Labor

Millet, Bonheur and the painters of rural France, 1849–1884

After the Revolution of 1848, peasants and farm work became a major subject. Some painters gave farm labor dignity and grandeur; others showed its toll, and were accused of politics or ugliness.

  • Millet's sower, gleaners, angelus, hoe and rake
  • Rosa Bonheur's working animals
  • Breton's and Bastien-Lepage's later naturalism
