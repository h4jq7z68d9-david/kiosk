Realism in France

Gustave Courbet

The Painter's Studio

1854–55

Oil on canvas, 361 × 598 cm. Musée d'Orsay, Paris

Courbet paints a landscape at the center of a crowded studio.

  • Its full title calls it a "real allegory" of seven years of his artistic life.
  • On the right are friends and backers, among them Baudelaire reading; a hunter on the left may have Napoleon III's features.
  • From 1899 to 1919 it hung as the backdrop of a private amateur theater in Paris.
  • He turns his own studio and career into the subject of a monumental picture.
