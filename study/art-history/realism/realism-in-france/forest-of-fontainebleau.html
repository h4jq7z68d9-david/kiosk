Realism in France

Camille Corot

Forest of Fontainebleau

1834

Oil on canvas, 175.6 × 242.6 cm. National Gallery of Art, Washington

A large forest view, painted for the Salon of 1834 from sketches made outdoors.

  • It was still framed as a historic landscape: viewers took the woman reading in the foreground for Mary Magdalene.
  • The National Gallery notes the "clarity and freshness of vision" that set Corot apart from formula landscapes.
  • The forest, not the small figure, holds the eye.
Salon
the official annual or biennial art exhibition in Paris, where artists built their reputations.
Historic landscape
a landscape with a story from history, myth or the Bible, a category meant to raise landscape's status.
