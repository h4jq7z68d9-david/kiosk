Gustave Courbet

A Burial at Ornans

1849–50

French, 1819–1877. Oil on canvas, 124 × 263 in. Musée d'Orsay, Paris

124 × 263 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Courbet painted ordinary villagers at life size, on a canvas about 22 feet wide, in heavy paint and deep blacks. The mourners' heads run in one long row at nearly the same height, so no single figure takes the lead.

  • A funeral in Courbet's hometown of Ornans, with townspeople as the sitters; shown at the Salon of 1850–51.
