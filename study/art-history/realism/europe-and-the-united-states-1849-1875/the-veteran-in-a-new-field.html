Winslow Homer

The Veteran in a New Field

1865

American, 1836–1910. Oil on canvas, 24 1/4 × 38 1/4 in. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

24 1/4 × 38 1/4 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Homer reduced the picture to three bands: blue sky, a wall of golden wheat, and cut stalks in front. The wheat rises to one nearly straight edge with no distance behind it, so it reads as a flat wall.

  • Painted after the Civil War ended; the discarded Union jacket and canteen show that the farmer is a returned soldier.
