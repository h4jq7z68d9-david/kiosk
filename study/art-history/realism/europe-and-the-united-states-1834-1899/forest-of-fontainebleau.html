Jean-Baptiste-Camille Corot

Forest of Fontainebleau

1834

Oil on canvas, 69 1/4 × 95 1/2 in. National Gallery of Art, Washington

Jean-Baptiste-Camille Corot (French, 1796–1875). Browns, olive greens and dull golds fill a real forest with no grand ruin or classical temple.

Look at

  • Light falls in patches on a sandy bank in the middle distance.
  • The trees at the right are a dark mass, with single leaves picked out in light.
  • The still, dark pool reflects the overhanging rock.
  • The figure in the lower left is small, a light accent in a large landscape.
  • Made in the studio from sketches and studies painted outdoors, and shown at the Salon, the official exhibition of the French Academy.
