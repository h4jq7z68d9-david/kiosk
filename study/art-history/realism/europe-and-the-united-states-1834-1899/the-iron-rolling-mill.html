Adolph Menzel

The Iron Rolling Mill

1872–75

Oil on canvas, 62 1/4 × 100 in. Alte Nationalgalerie, Berlin

Adolph Menzel (German, 1815–1905). Deep dark fills the factory, broken by a white-hot glow at the center and smaller fires behind.

Look at

  • Light from the hot iron falls on faces, arms and tongs nearby.
  • Beams, belts and wheels form a dark web overhead.
  • Workers at the edges wash and eat in near darkness.
  • Brushwork is quick and broken, with sparks as dabs of yellow.
  • Industrial labor, painted at a size once kept for history painting. The painter drew at a works in Upper Silesia in 1872.
