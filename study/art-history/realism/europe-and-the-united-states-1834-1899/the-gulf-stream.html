Winslow Homer

The Gulf Stream

1899

Oil on canvas, 28 1/4 × 49 1/4 in. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Short, broken strokes of teal, navy and white build a rough sea around a small boat tilted on a diagonal.

Look at

  • Sharks circle in the foreground as grey and white shapes.
  • A waterspout rises at the right as a pale grey funnel.
  • A ship sits tiny and faint on the horizon at the left.
  • The broken stump of the mast juts from the bow at the left.
  • A Black man faces danger alone on a damaged boat. The painter reworked the canvas by 1906, the year the museum bought it.
