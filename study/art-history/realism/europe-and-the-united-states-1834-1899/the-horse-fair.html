Rosa Bonheur

The Horse Fair

1852–55

Oil on canvas, 96 1/4 × 199 1/2 in. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Rosa Bonheur (French, 1822–1899). A stream of horses surges from left to right.

Look at

  • White horses at the center stand out against dark and chestnut ones.
  • Two horses rear at the center, a black one behind a white one, at the highest point of the moving line.
  • Muscles, hooves and manes are painted in close detail.
  • Dust rises from the dirt road and softens the legs and shadows.
  • A dark band of trees and a stormy sky hold the top of the picture.
  • A horse market on a Paris boulevard. The painting first appeared at the Salon of 1853 and was retouched by the artist in 1855.
