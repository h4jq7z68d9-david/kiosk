Jules Breton

The Song of the Lark

1884

Oil on canvas, 43 1/2 × 33 3/4 in. Art Institute of Chicago

Jules Breton (French, 1827–1906). One upright figure fills most of the height, her head above a low horizon.

Look at

  • A red sun low on the horizon sends warm light across the field.
  • The face is lifted and lit, the clearest detail.
  • Bare feet and the dusty path are painted closely.
  • The sky fades from pink-orange to pale yellow.
  • A band of green field separates the bare ground from the village.
  • A peasant girl stops work to hear a lark, painted in an idealized style.
