Édouard Manet

The Old Musician

1862

Oil on canvas, 73 3/4 × 97 3/4 in. National Gallery of Art, Washington

Édouard Manet (French, 1832–1883). Figures are broad, flat shapes with little modeling.

Look at

  • The figures stand and sit in a loose row and barely look at one another.
  • The boy's white shirt and pale trousers are the brightest area among the figures.
  • The ground and sky are brushed in broad, open strokes.
  • A tree at the upper left and the cloaked figure at the far right close the row.
  • The musician's red violin is the strongest single color.
  • The museum says the canvas shows homeless and marginalized people.
