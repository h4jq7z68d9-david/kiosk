Ilya Repin

Barge Haulers on the Volga

1870–73

Oil on canvas, 51 3/4 × 110 3/4 in. State Russian Museum, St Petersburg

Ilya Repin (1844–1930). Eleven men in harness form one long chain from the left toward the ship at the right.

Look at

  • Most heads bend down and forward under the strain of the harness.
  • One young figure near the center stands upright, the lightest and clearest face.
  • Ragged clothes are browns, greys and dull reds against pale sand.
  • The sand and sky are bright and flat, so the dark group stands out.
  • The ship's mast rises far above the bent heads.
  • The figures are based on portraits of real haulers, drawn on two trips to the Volga.
