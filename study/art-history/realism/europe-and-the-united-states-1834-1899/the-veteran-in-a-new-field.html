Winslow Homer

The Veteran in a New Field

1865

Oil on canvas, 24 1/4 × 38 1/4 in. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Winslow Homer (American, 1836–1910). The picture is three bands: blue sky, a wall of golden wheat and cut stalks in front.

Look at

  • One figure, seen from behind, is set against the wheat.
  • Cut stalks are quick strokes of yellow and white that fan out across the foreground.
  • The curved blade of the scythe is a dark line across the gold.
  • A dark jacket and canteen lie on the ground at the lower right.
  • Completed after the end of the Civil War. The discarded Union jacket and canteen show that the farmer is a returned soldier.
