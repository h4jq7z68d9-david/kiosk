Thomas Eakins

The Champion Single Sculls (Max Schmitt in a Single Scull)

1871

Oil on canvas, 32 1/4 × 46 1/4 in. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Thomas Eakins (American, 1844–1916). Clear, even daylight shows every detail at every distance.

Look at

  • The river is a smooth mirror of the sky and trees.
  • The bridges far behind are drawn as sharply as the boat in front.
  • The yellow scull and its oars make long, thin lines across the water.
  • Late-autumn trees are rust and brown against a cool blue sky.
  • A second rower sits in the middle distance, small and in the same clear light.
  • Believed to mark a rowing win by Max Schmitt in 1870. The painter shows himself as the rower in the middle distance.
