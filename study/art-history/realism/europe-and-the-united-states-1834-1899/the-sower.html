Jean-François Millet

The Sower

1850

Oil on canvas, 40 × 32 1/2 in. Museum of Fine Arts, Boston

Jean-François Millet (French, 1814–1875). The face is lost in shadow under the hat, and the body is a dark mass against a grey sky.

Look at

  • One heavy figure strides downhill and fills almost the whole canvas.
  • Earth browns cover the hillside and the shirt.
  • The blue trousers are the strongest color against the browns.
  • The far horizon glows pale yellow behind a tiny team of oxen.
  • The striding legs form a wide diagonal, which gives the figure its forward push.
  • The figure scatters wheat seed from a bag slung over the shoulder.
