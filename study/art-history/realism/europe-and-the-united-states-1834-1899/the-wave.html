Gustave Courbet

The Wave

1869–70

Oil on canvas, 25 3/4 × 36 1/2 in. Städel Museum, Frankfurt am Main

Thick paint is pushed and scraped on, so the wave looks heavy and solid.

Look at

  • The wave is a dark green wall across the middle of the canvas.
  • Foam is laid on in thick white patches.
  • Rocks in the foreground are red-brown blocks of paint.
  • The sky is a low ceiling of brown-grey cloud with no blue.
  • No boat or figure gives a sense of scale.
  • One of about sixty pictures of storm waves on the Normandy coast, made between 1865 and 1870.
