Classical Myth in Art

The stories of the Greek and Roman gods and heroes, told through paintings, sculpture, and vases

Each page pairs one work of art with a short account of the myth it shows, what the object was made for, and the key names and terms. Theme sections step back to the bigger ideas that connect the works. The material follows a college survey of classical mythology, rewritten for general readers.
