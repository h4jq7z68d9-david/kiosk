France, 1884–1906

Post-Impressionism

Structure, feeling, flat colour and pattern after Impressionism

Post-Impressionism is the label given, after the fact, to painters working in France from about 1884 to 1906 who started from Impressionism and went past it. They kept its bright colour and visible brushwork, and used them for something other than recording light: solid structure, strong feeling, flat colour, or pattern.
