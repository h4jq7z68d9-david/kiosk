Realism

Everyday life, painted as it looks

Realism is a nineteenth-century movement that painted subjects from everyday life in a naturalistic way. Before it, Western art was led by the academic ideal of history painting. The French novelist Champfleury coined the name in the 1840s, and the painter Gustave Courbet became the movement's best-known example.
