Myths explain the world

Many myths were the ancient answer to questions that science answers today.

Why do volcanoes rumble? Because Zeus pinned the monster Typhon under Mount Etna. Why does winter come? Because Hades carried Persephone to the underworld and her mother Demeter grieves while she is gone. Stories like these, which explain how something came to be, are called etiological myths, and they run through almost every story in this guide.

  • Etiology = explanation of a cause or origin
  • Seasons: Persephone’s stay with Hades
  • Volcanoes: Typhon trapped under Etna
Etiological tale
A myth that explains the origin of a natural event, custom, or ritual; many divine myths do this.
