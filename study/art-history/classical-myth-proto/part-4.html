The Origins of Mortals

Prometheus, Pandora, the Five Ages, and the Flood

The Greeks had no single story of how humans were made; Hesiod never says. Later writers credit the Titan Prometheus with shaping people from clay, and every version agrees that he became humankind’s champion against Zeus. His tricks cost humans dearly: Zeus answered with Pandora, the first woman, and the stories go on to explain toil, sickness, marriage, and a long decline from a golden age.

  • Prometheus shapes and protects humans
  • Zeus’s answer: Pandora and her jar
  • Decline through five ages; the flood
Prometheus
Titan whose name means “forethought”; trickster who sides with humans, steals fire, and is punished by Zeus.
Epimetheus
Prometheus’s brother, “afterthought,” who accepts Pandora despite being warned against gifts from Zeus.
