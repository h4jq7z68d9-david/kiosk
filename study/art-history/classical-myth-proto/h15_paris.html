Peter Paul Rubens

The Judgement of Paris

ca. 1632–35

Oil on panel · National Gallery, London

Made for: A large late painting by Rubens of a favorite subject for showing three ideal female nudes.

Zeus refused to decide which goddess deserved Eris’s apple and sent Hermes to ask the Trojan prince Paris, a son of King Priam and Hecuba, who was tending sheep on Mount Ida. Hera offered power, Athena victory in war, and Aphrodite the most beautiful woman in the world. Paris chose Aphrodite, and his prize was Helen, the wife of Menelaus of Sparta. Rubens shows Paris holding the apple as the three goddesses pose before him.

  • Hera, Athena, Aphrodite compete
  • Paris chooses Aphrodite and Helen
  • The cause of the Trojan War
