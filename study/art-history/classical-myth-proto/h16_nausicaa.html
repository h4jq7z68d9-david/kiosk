Pieter Lastman

Odysseus and Nausicaa

1619

Oil on panel · Alte Pinakothek, Munich

Made for: A history painting by Rembrandt’s teacher; its first owner is not recorded.

Shipwrecked and naked, Odysseus appeared before Princess Nausicaa and her maids as they washed clothes by the shore. Nausicaa kept calm and led him to her father’s palace, where the seafaring Phaeacians welcomed him, heard his story, and carried him home to Ithaca. Lastman shows the maids scattering in fright while the princess holds her ground.
