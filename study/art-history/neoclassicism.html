Neoclassicism

Rome, Paris and London, from the 1760s to the first years of Napoleon

Neoclassicism is the return to the clear lines, calm order and stern subjects of ancient Greece and Rome, from the painters working in Rome in the 1760s to David's Paris around the Revolution.
