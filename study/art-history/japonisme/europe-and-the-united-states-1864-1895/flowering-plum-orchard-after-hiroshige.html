Vincent van Gogh

Flowering Plum Orchard (after Hiroshige)

1887

Dutch, 1853–1890. Oil on canvas, 22 × 18 1/2 in. Van Gogh Museum, Amsterdam

22 × 18 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Van Gogh copied a Japanese print in oil paint and made its colors stronger, especially the red sky and green grass. He added orange borders with large dark green characters, so strong color runs to the edge of the canvas.

  • The first of three paintings he made after prints in his own collection, here Hiroshige's Plum Garden in Kameido.
