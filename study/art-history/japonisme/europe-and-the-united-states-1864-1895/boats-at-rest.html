Arthur Wesley Dow

Boats at Rest

about 1895

American, 1857–1922. Oil on canvas, 26 × 36 in. Art Institute of Chicago

26 × 36 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Dow showed the rowboats from above and let the left edge cut off a shed. The river curves up toward a high horizon, so the ground seems to tilt and the boats' insides are in full view.

  • Through his books and teaching, Dow's ideas on composition shaped many Arts and Crafts designers.
