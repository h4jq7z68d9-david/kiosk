Félix Vallotton

The Protest

1893

Swiss French, 1865–1925. Woodcut, image 8 × 12 1/2 in. The Cleveland Museum of Art

8 × 12 1/2 in., beside a person 5 ft 8 in tall

Vallotton set solid black figures racing across bare paper, seen from above. The empty lower-right corner makes the crowd look packed into the top of the sheet.

  • Police are breaking up a political demonstration.
