Vincent van Gogh

Flowering Plum Orchard (after Hiroshige)

1887

Oil on canvas, 22 × 18 1/2 in. Van Gogh Museum, Amsterdam

Vincent van Gogh (1853–1890). Strong red, green and orange fill a painted copy of a Japanese print of plum trees in bloom.

Look at

  • A dark trunk crosses the picture on a diagonal in front of a garden of white blossom.
  • The sky shades from red through pink to pale yellow.
  • Two orange borders hold large characters painted in dark green.
  • The trunk is reddish brown with blue-black shadow.
  • The first of three paintings he made after prints in his own collection, here Hiroshige's Plum Garden in Kameido. He followed the design closely, strengthened the color and added the borders.
