Félix Vallotton

The Protest

1893

Woodcut, image 8 × 12 1/2 in. The Cleveland Museum of Art

Félix Vallotton (Swiss French, 1865–1925). Solid black figures race across white paper, seen from above.

Look at

  • The street tilts up steeply toward the buildings at the top left.
  • The empty lower-right corner takes up about a third of the sheet.
  • Checks and dots break up a few of the black coats.
  • The figures are flat shapes with almost no modeling.
  • The museum links the flat space, tipped-up view and pattern to Japanese color woodcuts. Police are breaking up a political demonstration.
