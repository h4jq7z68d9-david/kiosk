Vincent van Gogh

Almond Blossom

1890

Oil on canvas, 28 3/4 × 36 1/2 in. Van Gogh Museum, Amsterdam

Almond branches in bloom spread across the canvas, seen close and from below, with the edges cutting them off.

Look at

  • The blue background is flat, with visible brushwork but little depth.
  • Branches are drawn with dark outlines.
  • Blossoms are thick dabs of white and pale pink.
  • The trunk enters from the bottom edge, and the frame crops the rest of the tree.
  • The museum says the choice of subject, the strong outlines and the tree's position in the picture are borrowed from Japanese printmaking. A gift for his brother Theo on the birth of Theo's son.
