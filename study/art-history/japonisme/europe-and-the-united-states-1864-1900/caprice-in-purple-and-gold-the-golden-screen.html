James McNeill Whistler

Caprice in Purple and Gold: The Golden Screen

1864

Oil on wood panel, 19 3/4 × 27 in. Freer Gallery of Art, Smithsonian, Washington

James McNeill Whistler (1834–1903). A gold folding screen fills the back of the room behind a model in purple and red robes.

Look at

  • The woman sits low on the floor, and her robes spread out toward the lower right.
  • Strong red lining stands out between the purple kimono and the white under-robe.
  • Prints lie scattered on the floor at the right while she holds another up to read.
  • Buildings and small figures on the screen are brushed loosely in green and grey over the gold.
  • The model, Joanna Hiffernan, poses as a Japanese courtesan, studying a print from Hiroshige's series Views of the Sixty-odd Provinces.
