Paul Gauguin

Volpini Suite: Laundresses (Les Laveuses)

1889

French, 1848–1903. Zincograph on yellow paper, image 8 1/4 × 10 1/4 in. The Cleveland Museum of Art

Gauguin printed black lines and dark masses on bright yellow paper and showed the bending woman from behind and above.

  • Froth is drawn as looping lines with little shading, so the water reads as a flat pattern rising up the sheet.
  • The border cuts off a cow at lower left, so it looks close and just below eye level, strengthening the downward view.
  • The museum points to the strange viewpoint, the stylized foam and the cropped cow as signs of Japanese woodblock prints. One of his first prints, drawn on zinc plates.
