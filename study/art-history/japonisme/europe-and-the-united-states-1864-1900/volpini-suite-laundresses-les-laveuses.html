Paul Gauguin

Volpini Suite: Laundresses (Les Laveuses)

1889

Zincograph on yellow paper, image 8 1/4 × 10 1/4 in. The Cleveland Museum of Art

Paul Gauguin (French, 1848–1903). Bright yellow paper carries the black lines and dark masses of the print.

Look at

  • A woman bends over the water, seen from behind and above.
  • Froth on the water is drawn as wavy, looping lines.
  • A cow at the lower left is cut off by the border.
  • Dark areas are dense crayon tone set flat against the yellow.
  • The museum points to the strange viewpoint, the stylized foam and the cropped cow as signs of Japanese woodblock prints. One of his first prints, drawn on zinc plates.
