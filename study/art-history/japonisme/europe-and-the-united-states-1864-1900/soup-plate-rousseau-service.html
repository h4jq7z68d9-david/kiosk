Félix Bracquemond

Soup Plate, Rousseau Service

1866–75

Creamware, diameter 9 3/4 in. The Metropolitan Museum of Art, New York

Félix Bracquemond (French, 1833–1914). A turkey sits just off-center and a moth, an insect and a flowering sprig sit toward the rim, all drawn in dark outline on the white plate.

Look at

  • Wide white space surrounds every motif.
  • Color comes in a few touches: orange on the head, pink on the breast, pale green leaves.
  • Feathers are rows of small scalloped marks.
  • A ragged band of blue-violet edges the rim.
  • Bracquemond owned Hokusai's Manga, a many-volume book of sketches, earlier than most Europeans, and his designs for the service draw on it.
