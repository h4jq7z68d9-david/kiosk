Pierre Bonnard

Nursemaids' Promenade, Frieze of Carriages

1895

Color lithographs, four-panel screen, each panel 54 1/4 × 18 3/4 in. The Cleveland Museum of Art

Pierre Bonnard (French, 1867–1947). Four tall panels, made to stand as a folding screen, carry one scene from left to right.

Look at

  • A row of carriages runs along the top like a frieze.
  • Much of each panel is bare paper.
  • The nanny's cape is a flat brown shape with few inner lines.
  • A hoop drawn as a thin circle floats in the second panel.
  • The museum says Bonnard copied the flat blocks of color in the Japanese color woodcuts he collected.
