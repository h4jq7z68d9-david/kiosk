Exekias

Dionysus Cup

c. 530 BCE

Black-figure terracotta, 30.5 cm diameter · Staatliche Antikensammlungen, Munich

An Athenian wine cup signed by Exekias, the leading black-figure artist of his day. It was exported to Etruria and found in a tomb at Vulci.

  • Dionysus reclines in a ship whose mast sprouts a vine with seven bunches of grapes.
  • Seven dolphins circle the ship, the pirates he transformed.
  • A coral-red ground fills the whole interior, sea and sky in one.
  • Outside, big eyes on each side and warriors fighting over a fallen body.
  • When a drinker tipped the cup, the picture appeared from under the wine.

The subject

Pirates kidnapped Dionysus, not knowing he was a god. He filled the ship with vines and turned them into dolphins as they leapt overboard.

Kylix
A shallow two-handled drinking cup used at the symposium, the Greek drinking party.
