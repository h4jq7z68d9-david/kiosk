The Gods of Olympus

Who the gods are, and how artists made each one recognizable

The Greeks pictured their gods as a family of twelve living on Mount Olympus, and the Romans took them over under Latin names. Because the gods look human, artists marked each one with a fixed set of objects, animals and poses, so a viewer could name the god at a glance.

  • Ancient statues set the standard types that later painters copied.
  • Renaissance and Baroque artists revived the gods for princely palaces and collections.
  • Learn the attributes here and the rest of the guide becomes easier to read.
Attribute
An object, animal or feature that identifies a figure, such as Zeus’s thunderbolt or Athena’s owl and aegis.
