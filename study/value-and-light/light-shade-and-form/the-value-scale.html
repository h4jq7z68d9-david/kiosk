Light, Shade and Form

The Value Scale

Value means how light or dark something is, whatever its color, and a value scale lays it out in steps from white to black.

Look at

  • Ten bands step from paper white at the left to solid black at the right, each a shade darker than the last.
  • Merrifield pointed out that each flat band looks darker along the edge where it meets a lighter one.

Jean-Auguste-Dominique Ingres

Odalisque in Grisaille, about 1824–34

Ingres modeled this unfinished, smaller repeat of his 1814 Grande Odalisque almost entirely in grays, so value does nearly all the work of building the body.

Merrifield noticed that on a lit object most of the surface sits in the middle values, with only small patches at either extreme.
