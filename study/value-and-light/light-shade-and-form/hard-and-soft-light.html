Light, Shade and Form

Hard and Soft Light

The smaller a light looks from where the subject stands, the harder it is: the distant sun gives hard light, a cloudy sky or a big nearby window soft light.

Look at

  • Top: in direct sun the shadows have crisp edges.
  • Bottom: weak light arriving from several sides wraps around the forms, and the faint shadows fade at their edges.

Joaquín Sorolla

Sewing the Sail, 1896

Sunshine broken up by the leaves spatters the white sail with crisp spots of light and shade.

Outdoors, Cross observed, light bouncing in from every side makes shadows lighter than they are indoors.
