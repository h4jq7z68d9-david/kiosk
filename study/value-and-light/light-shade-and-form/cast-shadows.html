Light, Shade and Form

Cast Shadows

When an object stands in the light's path, it throws its shape onto nearby surfaces as a cast shadow.

Look at

  • Each cube throws a band of shadow across the table on the side away from the light.
  • Each cast shadow is darker than the shaded side of the cube that throws it.

Vilhelm Hammershøi

Dust Motes Dancing in the Sunbeams, 1901

The window bars block parts of the sunbeams, so the patches of sun on the floor carry the window's grid of shadow.

Cast shadows are usually darkest and crispest next to the object, and which way they point and how far they stretch reveal where the light sits.
