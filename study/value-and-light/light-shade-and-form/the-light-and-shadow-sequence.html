The Light and Shadow Sequence

A rounded form turns away from a single light bit by bit, so its values run in a set order: highlight, light side, halftone (a middle tone), darkest shadow band, reflected light bounced into the shadow, then the shadow on the ground.

Image: Internet Archive · Public domain (published before 1931)

Sánchez Cotán let the lit fronts of the quince and cabbage give way to deep shadow on the sides away from the light, so each looks round against the dark opening.
