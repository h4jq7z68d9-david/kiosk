Light, Shade and Form

Highlights

On a polished surface, a highlight is a small reflected image of the light source.

Look at

  • The dark bottle shows two small highlights, one for each half of the divided window it faced.
  • The pale top of the block shows in the bottle's glaze as a curved reflected shape.

Willem Claesz Heda

Still Life with Oysters, a Silver Tazza, and Glassware, 1635

Tiny, crisp highlights tell us the tall glass and the tipped silver cup are polished; the oysters' wet shine is broader and softer.

The Met points to a gleaming spoon that leads the eye on to a shard of glass and other curving forms.
