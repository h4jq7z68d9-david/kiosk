Light in Painting

Leonardo da Vinci

The Virgin of the Rocks

about 1491/2–9 and 1506–8

Oil on wood, 189.5 × 120 cm. The National Gallery, London

Out of the dark grotto the figures appear by soft degrees; the Virgin's temples and nose have no hard outline.

  • A shaft of light enters from the upper left.
  • The far mountains are painted blue, with softened edges, so they look hazy and distant.
