Leonardo's Relief

Leonardo da Vinci treated light and shade as the way a flat picture gains relief: the look of solid, rounded form.

He studied how light falls on faces and folds of cloth and wrote about it at length.

  • Studies of drapery like this one let him work out light on folds in detail.
  • In 1897 the drawing teacher Anson Cross reproduced this study to show the care great artists took over light and shade.
