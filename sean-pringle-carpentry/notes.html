How this mockup works

This is a draft of your website for you to react to. It isn’t live, and customers can’t find it.

What’s real and what isn’t

  • The layout, the pages, and the way it works are the proposal.
  • The photos are stock pictures, not your work. Yours will replace them.
  • The wording, the jobs, the sizes, and the phone number are examples.

How a customer would use it

  • Home shows a banner photo, a short introduction, and your recent jobs.
  • Work shows every job. Customers can switch between Indoor and Outdoor, and tap any photo to see it larger.
  • Each job has one main photo and a few close-ups that show the detail.
  • About is where your own words go, with how a job runs and where you work.
  • Request an estimate takes them to a short form at the bottom of any page. In this mockup the form doesn’t send anything. On the real site, what they fill in reaches you by email.
  • The plan is for each request to show up in your expenses page too, where you can turn it into a job.
  • Your phone number is on every page, and it dials when tapped on a phone.

What we need from you

  • Photos of finished jobs. For each job: one wide shot and two or three close-ups of the detail.
  • A line or two per job: what it was, the wood, the rough size, and how long it took.
  • A few sentences about yourself, in your own words.
  • Your phone number and the towns you work in.
  • One wide photo for the top of the home page.
