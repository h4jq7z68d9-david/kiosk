About Sean

Sean has worked as a carpenter in Colorado for most of his adult life. He started on framing crews, moved to finish work, and now takes on the jobs where the details show: trim, built-ins, stairs, decks, and pergolas.

He works alone or with one helper, so the person who measures your room is the person who builds it. He would rather take a week longer than leave a joint he isn’t happy with.

How a job goes

  1. Sean visits, looks at the space, and measures.
  2. You get a written estimate with the materials and the time it should take.
  3. He schedules the work and tells you what to clear out beforehand.
  4. He builds it, cleans up, and walks through the finished work with you.

Where he works

Fort Collins, Loveland, Windsor, Greeley, and the towns between. Ask about jobs farther out.
